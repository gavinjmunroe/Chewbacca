Firm Foundation's site (firmfoundation.tech, site/ in the firm-foundation repo) has three
panels in the pinned what-we-do scene, and each moves a different way. Caleb and Gavin set
this on their 2026-10-17 call: the audit grid is scroll driven (red X marks pop per church),
follow-up is click driven (three numbered circles, the live one an oval), and the back end
loops on a timer (one layer lit at a time). Keep it that way when editing any of the three.

The Monday section is the one Apple-style part (rounded, plain white, real icons); the rest
of the site stays square per site/reference/DESIGN.md. The footer is one short bar, the hero
shows five plates on laptops and three on phones, and nothing on the site may read like a
slideshow or a spreadsheet. Built in gavinjmunroe/firm-foundation PR #1.
