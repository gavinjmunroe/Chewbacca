Caleb, 2026-10-28, while pricing the UCLA showcase founders (Junto, OPTRA, Firebox): at BMA, Joe's and Roy's accounts ran roughly $2K to $4K a month, and Sagar's big clients paid $20K+ a month.

**How to apply:** this is the market Caleb has seen with his own eyes, so price founder retainers against it, not against a guess. Size the ask to the client. OPTRA's own plans are $39 to $129 a month, so a $20K retainer doesn't fit a student-run startup. The BMA $2K to $4K band is the ceiling for small founders until a pilot proves meetings. A price still needs Caleb and Gavin to agree before it goes in a call script.
