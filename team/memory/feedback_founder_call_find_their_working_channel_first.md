Before a founder call, the prep names the channel the founder is actually getting results from (cold email, LinkedIn, inbound, conferences) and builds the pilot for that channel. When no note says, the first question on the call asks it, and the prep carries a version of the gift for each likely channel.

**Why:** 2026-10-28, Reed Watne (Junto). Every piece of prep was a cold email bake-off against Apollo: 164 MSP owners, email copy, a send gate demo. Two minutes in, Reed said he ran cold email from January on 10 domains and 50 mailboxes, got almost no leads, doesn't believe in it, and now gets his results from LinkedIn through Dripify, plus inbound content and conferences. The call was saved by moving the same list onto his Dripify live. The prep could have known: he had described his go-to-market at the UCLA showcase.

**How to apply:**

- Read every earlier transcript and note on the founder for how they get customers now, before choosing the deliverable.
- Make the list channel-neutral: a LinkedIn profile URL on every row as well as an email. Junto's 164 rows had no LinkedIn URLs, so the pilot he said yes to could not start that day.
- Open discovery with "What's working for you right now, and what isn't?" and let the answer pick the pilot.
- Most founders aren't Reed. Caleb, 10/28: "Junto is def very diff bc they alr had tried outbound w appollo. Firebox and many other clients dont even know the power of outbound." For them, open by SHOWING one real message to one real prospect and what a yes would mean, before any pilot ask.
