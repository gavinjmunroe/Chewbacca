Anchored OS is the plan to turn Anchored (the Track 3 build from the FaithTech x Baptize The World hackathon, 2026-10-24, github.com/calebnewtonusc/anchored) into an ecosystem for church staff, ministers and any Christian. Caleb proposed it on 2026-10-24 as something he and Gavin build in parallel with the B2B GTME for [[project_firm_foundation_founding_2026_10_09]]. He asked that every idea from the day's demos be folded in.

Plan repo: github.com/calebnewtonusc/anchored-os (private, Gavin invited). IDEAS.md has 58 ideas from 8 teams, each credited, and ECOSYSTEM.md maps them to one engine with nine products: Onboard, Gather, Count, Anchor, Testify, Share, Connect, Walk (the Mac layer) and Oversee. Nothing is decided. OPEN-QUESTIONS.md holds the calls: Firm Foundation's "no AI pastoral care" rule against a daily companion, Ogo and Anthony co-owning Anchored, crediting other teams, channel order, and church vs movement as the buyer.
