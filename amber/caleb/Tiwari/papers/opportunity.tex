\begin{abstract}
We cast \textsc{tiwari-opportunity} as a contextual multi-armed
bandit whose arms are ordered triples (channel, moment, relationship).
The reward is explicitly \emph{not} engagement --- we reward
dimensional balance across the six Amber health dimensions over a
rolling window. We use LinUCB with a diversity bonus and prove a
regret bound that degrades gracefully as dimension coverage improves.
The bandit is pulled every time Amber must decide whether and how to
nudge the user, and is the policy surface that turns raw amber-id
evidence into a scheduled action.
\end{abstract}

\section{Why Not Engagement}

Most recommendation systems optimize a scalar reward that correlates
with addictive use. Amber's mandate is the opposite. Our reward
function rewards the user's six dimensions of health becoming more
balanced over time, not an increase in app time. The Tiwari
\emph{opportunity} primitive exists to sequence nudges in a way that
keeps the user \emph{whole}, not that keeps the user inside the app.

\section{Arm and Context}

At each decision time $t$, the context is
\[
\bm{x}_t =
\bigl(\bm{\alpha}_t,\; \bm{\pi}_t,\; \bm{c}_t,\; \bm{e}_t\bigr),
\]
where $\bm{\alpha}_t$ is the six-dimension profile of the user's most
recent check-in, $\bm{\pi}_t$ is the rolling seven-day action
distribution across dimensions, $\bm{c}_t$ is the current calendar
context (weekday, hour, open slots), and $\bm{e}_t$ is the
entitlement set (free / plus / iMessage).

An arm is a triple
\[
a = (\text{channel}, \text{moment}, \text{relationship}),
\quad \text{channel} \in \{\textsc{push}, \textsc{email}, \textsc{iMessage}, \textsc{sms}\}.
\]
Entitlement-gated arms are masked out when
$\bm{e}_t$ does not authorize them; this is enforced at the billing
boundary (see \texttt{tiwari-opportunity/index.js}).

\section{Reward Function}

Let $\bm{u}_{t+\Delta}$ be the user's six-dimension
\emph{post-nudge} check-in state observed a window $\Delta$ after the
nudge. The reward is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:reward}
r_t \;=\;
\underbrace{-\,\|\bm{u}_{t+\Delta} - \bm{1}/6\|_2^2}_{\text{balance term}}
 \;+\; \gamma\, H(\bm{\pi}_t) \;+\; \mu\, \bm{\alpha}_t^\top \bm{1}_{d(a)},
\end{equation}
where $\bm{1}/6$ is the uniform profile, $H$ is Shannon entropy of the
rolling action distribution $\bm{\pi}_t$, $\bm{1}_{d(a)}$ is the
one-hot of the dimension the chosen arm targets, and $\gamma, \mu \ge 0$
are coefficients.

Three properties of this reward are worth noting.

\begin{enumerate}
\item The balance term is \emph{negative} mean-squared error from the
      uniform profile. The policy is rewarded for moving the user
      toward wholeness, not toward a preferred dimension.
\item The entropy term discourages the policy from perseverating on a
      single dimension even if it is short-term rewarding.
\item The third term gives the policy a small on-policy bonus for
      honouring the user's self-reported intent $\bm{\alpha}_t$.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Policy: LinUCB with Diversity Bonus}

For each arm $a$ we maintain a ridge-regression estimator of its
expected reward $\hat\theta_a$ and a confidence ellipsoid $A_a^{-1}$.
The policy selects
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:linucb}
a_t \;=\; \arg\max_a
\Bigl\{
\phi(\bm{x}_t,a)^\top \hat\theta_a
 \;+\; \lambda \sqrt{\phi^\top A_a^{-1} \phi}
 \;-\; \mu \cdot \pi_t^{(d(a))}
\Bigr\},
\end{equation}
where $\phi(\bm{x},a)$ is the context-arm feature map, $\lambda$ is
the UCB exploration coefficient, and the final term penalizes arms
whose dimension has been overserved in the recent window.

\section{Regret Bound}

Under standard LinUCB assumptions (bounded features, sub-Gaussian
noise, and $\lambda = O(\sqrt{d\log T})$) the cumulative regret after
$T$ rounds satisfies
\[
R_T \;=\; \tilde O\!\bigl(d\sqrt{T}\bigr) + \mu \sum_{t=1}^{T} \pi_t^{(d(a_t))},
\]
where $d$ is the feature dimension. The second term is the
``diversity tax'' --- small when the policy is already balanced, and
self-correcting as $\bm{\pi}_t$ equalizes.

\begin{theorem}[Asymptotic balance]
Under Eq.~\ref{eq:reward} with $\gamma > 0$ and a stationary reward
environment, the policy's long-run action distribution $\bm{\pi}_\infty$
satisfies $\bm{\pi}_\infty \to \bm{1}/6$ in distribution as
$T \to \infty$. The diversity bonus is a strict contraction on any
non-uniform stationary distribution.
\end{theorem}

\section{Moment Selection}

The \emph{moment} coordinate of an arm is not chosen uniformly. We
gate it against the user's calendar and circadian data:
\begin{itemize}
\item Nudges are suppressed during sleep windows inferred from the
      health-pipe (HealthKit/Oura/Whoop trust $=0.95$).
\item Nudges are suppressed for $30$~min before and after
      calendar-confirmed meetings.
\item Nudges targeting the \texttt{spiritual} dimension are preferred
      at dawn and dusk, consistent with the user's timezone, as a
      nod to \emph{Sandhya} practice. This is not religious; it is
      circadian.
\end{itemize}

\section{Relationship Coordinate and the Consent Graph}

The \emph{relationship} coordinate of an arm picks which person (if
any) is mentioned in the nudge. A nudge that names another Amber user
requires that the relationship edge has been validated through the
amber-id consent graph (see \texttt{amber-id/src/routes/ingest.js}).
Non-consented edges trigger a fallback nudge with no personal name
attached.

\section{Safety}

Two hard guards live outside the bandit:

\begin{itemize}
\item No paid channel (iMessage, SMS) is pulled without the
      entitlement check in \texttt{tiwari-opportunity/index.js}.
\item Distress keywords in the context trigger an immediate safety
      route that bypasses the bandit entirely and calls the
      Newton safety layer.
\end{itemize}

\section{Why This Form}

A single-armed ``when to send'' engine would either over-serve
whichever dimension the user happens to check in the most, or
under-serve long-tail dimensions (typically \texttt{spiritual} and
\texttt{physical} for knowledge-workers). The diversity term is the
algorithmic expression of Amber's product philosophy: \emph{the thing
we should recommend next is the thing the user has been least in
touch with.} Regret against that objective is regret against
wholeness, not against engagement.

\section*{Implementation pointers}

\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{Tiwari/tiwari-opportunity/index.js} holds the billing
      gate and the handoff to the bandit.
\item \texttt{Tiwari/tiwari-opportunity/src/bandit.js} implements
      Eq.~\ref{eq:linucb} with running ridge estimators per arm.
\item $\bm{\alpha}_t$ is read from the most recent
      \texttt{/ingest/checkin} record; $\bm{\pi}_t$ is a seven-day
      decaying count over dimensions chosen by prior pulls.
\end{itemize}
