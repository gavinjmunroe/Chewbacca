On the 10/31 call, Colin and Caleb set these rules for the Firm Foundation home page (site/, branch feat/hero-own-look):

- No object on Home is decoration. A floating drawing, hover toy or icon must stand for part of what FF does and link to it. Each plate now carries `is` / `href` / `says` in content.ts.
- The hero tells Matthew 7 in order:
  1. The sky goes near black in the storm.
  2. A section cut through the sand shows nothing underneath.
  3. The flood sweeps the apps to one side, where they float.
  4. The rock forms only after the sand is gone, as FF's work.
  5. FF calls the apps back in.
  6. The sky returns to blue, then white.
- Transitions are tied to the scroll, slightly delayed, with parallax layers. A plain gradient band reads as slop.
- Light and dark sections alternate. Ink on dull paper reads flat. Testimonials sit on near-black with white text and photos that trail the scroll. The team section sits on plain paper so the faces are the focus.

**Why:** Caleb on the old gradient: "the gradient is kind of slop". Colin: "make sure all the background objects have a purpose".

**How to apply:** Before adding any visual to an FF page, name the offering it stands for. If it has none, cut it. Related: [[feedback_ff_hero_not_lemma]].
