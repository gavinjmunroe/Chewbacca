Team Ignite Ventures passed on Amber on 2026-09-03 (letter to Sagar, Shirley, and
Karthik). Reasons: no named defensibility mechanism, and a closing vision that reads
as three companies. Four gates back to a yes, all four together over 3 to 6 months:
commit publicly to one positioning bet; launch one defined cohort and produce real
week 4 and week 8 retention for 200+ users; name and build the compounding
switching-cost or data-advantage mechanism; give full technical visibility including
Karthik's background and a working demo. Their update address is now
**updates@teamignite.ventures**.

**Why:** it is a soft pass with a real re-entry path, not a form letter, so the four
gates are a live checklist rather than closed history.

**How to apply:** gate 4 (demo + Karthik's background) is likely the biggest hidden
share of the pass and is cheapest to fix. Gate 2 is the only calendar-bound one: a
cohort launched by ~2026-09-22 puts week 8 in mid-November. Gate 3's answer is the
ranked personal graph (a private note), framed as a per-user data
advantage, never as lock-in, because [[project_amber_definition]] doctrine is
explicitly pro-portability. Full note: `projects/amber/fundraising.md`.
