Sagar's way is the standard for all outbound work. Before changing copy, a
list or a launch, read skills/gtm-engineering/references/sagar.md and ask what
he would do.

**Why:** Caleb, 2026-10-12: "NEVER DO SMTH SAGAR WOULDNT DO". That morning the
Zeutara drafts were rewritten to pass the GEX copy rubric (90/100), which
replaced the founder-story email 1 Sagar's method calls for with contrived,
grader-shaped lines ("a round you and [firm] may want to see", "one detail
[firm] may want on this round"). Asked what Sagar would change, the answer was
three things: 2 variants of 3 messages, a relationship column so Jonah's warm
investors get a text instead, and casual founder-story copy.

**How to apply:** email 1 opens on who it is from and what is sold, with the
founder's story told plainly; no grader-bait phrasing; 2 variants per offer;
check the sender's warm list before any cold send. Where GEX or any playbook
disagrees with him, he wins. gtme-grade passes his sender-first opener since
this date (tests/test_gtme_ready.py section 9).
