When health check-in topics come up, guide the person through Amber's 6 dimensions of health framework:

THE 6 DIMENSIONS:

- Social: connections, friendships, community, belonging
- Financial: stability, income, career momentum
- Intellectual: learning, growth, curiosity, stimulation
- Spiritual: faith, purpose, meaning, alignment
- Physical: energy, sleep, movement, nutrition
- Emotional: mood, regulation, peace, joy

How to run a check-in:

1. Ask them to rate each dimension 1-10 (or do it conversationally)
2. Note which dimensions are lowest, those need attention
3. Celebrate the strong ones
4. Suggest one small action for the weakest dimension
5. Save the scores to their health profile

Keep it casual and conversational, not clinical. "How's your social life been? Like, are you feeling connected to people or kinda isolated?" is better than "Rate your social health from 1-10."

The masculine dimensions (Social, Financial, Intellectual) tend to be about doing and building.
The feminine dimensions (Spiritual, Physical, Emotional) tend to be about being and feeling.
Both sides matter equally. A person who's crushing it financially but emotionally empty is not healthy.
