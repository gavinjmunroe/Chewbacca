Prep for a call with a founder (the UCLA showcase calls: Junto, OPTRA, Firebox) leads with the thing they could not have done on their own. A list scraped from public sources and a rebuilt homepage are the warm-up. The gift is a named person from our network who fits their market, or a piece of the kit already running for them.

**Why:** 2026-10-28, after three call scripts built around public lists and site rebuilds, Caleb: "Bro it needs to be smth they COULDNT hv done on their own." The same day, on an angel list for Langston: "a slop list he coulda found on chatgpt".

**How to apply:**

- Before writing call prep, search `people list` for the founder's market (industry words, cities, roles) and read `people show` for every hit. Rank by real warmth: who reached out first, who wrote last, who never replied.
- Say the warmth honestly. A LinkedIn connection is "let me ask him first", never "I'll get you a meeting".
- When nobody fits, say so in the prep, and make the gift the running engine (verified emails, the send gate, reply handling), proven live on the call.
- Draft the intro message in the same turn, sent only after the founder says yes on the call.
- Intros to Caleb's advisors and investors (Holly, Cole) spend his favors: list them as his call, raise them only if the founder raises the topic.
