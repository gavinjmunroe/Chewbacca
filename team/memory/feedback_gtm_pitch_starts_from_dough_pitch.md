Any client call or pitch about our outbound work starts from `PITCH.md` in calebnewtonusc/dough: a one-liner, five reasons each with receipts from several markets, answers to "how are you different from Apollo, Clay, HeyReach, by hand, Claude, an agency", the numbers we can and can't say, and the free 50 row ask.

**Why:** On 2026-10-30 Caleb pitched OPTRA solo and improvised, including "5 calls a day" off 200 emails a month and 20 to 30% reply rates with no source. Afterward: "I feel like I'm having to finesse words out my butt on the spot", and "remember how goated it is next time we ever talk anything gtm or client calls". A first version built only on OPTRA drew "Not j for Optra dumbass".

**How to apply:** Copy PITCH.md's lines into the call script, add one receipt from the prospect's market, name markets never clients, and add new receipts to PITCH.md the day they land. Our own reply rate stays off every page until a Dough campaign produces one.
