# Tizzy Executive Overview

**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** February 13, 2026
**Document Type:** Executive Summary

## One-Sentence Pitch

Tizzy is an always-wearable conversation coach that helps people improve real-world conversational outcomes by detecting the specific behaviors holding them back, then coaching those behaviors in the moment and across time—starting with the underserved autism market.

## The Problem

Many autistic individuals know they're experiencing social friction but cannot pinpoint what's going wrong in the moment. Key challenges:

- **Invisible Patterns:** Cannot see their own conversational behaviors in real-time
- **Delayed Feedback:** Post-conversation self-report is unreliable when cues were missed
- **Parental Blindness:** Parents cannot observe school/work conversations
- **Therapy Gap:** Weekly therapy sessions miss real-world context
- **Transition Overwhelm:** College, new jobs, and new schools trigger social overload

## The Solution

A discreet, always-wearable device (necklace form factor) that:

1. **Captures** real-world conversations with privacy controls
2. **Analyzes** acoustic signals (prosody, timing, tone) and conversational patterns
3. **Coaches** through subtle haptic feedback in the moment
4. **Reviews** post-conversation insights highlighting what worked and what to improve
5. **Tracks** long-term progress with measurable improvement metrics

## Strategic Wedge: Autism

**Why autism first?**
- **Underserved market** with high need and limited solutions
- **Clear pain point** around social communication differences
- **Measurable outcomes** aligned with clinical frameworks (ASHA benchmarks)
- **Dataset moat** - proprietary autism conversation data is defensible IP
- **Mission alignment** - builds trust and legitimacy as assistive technology

**Initial target:** College students and young adults during major life transitions

## Market Validation

The wearable AI conversation category is accelerating:
- **Amazon acquired Bee** (AI wristband for conversations) - confirms category viability
- **Ray-Ban Meta smartglasses** sold millions - proves form factor acceptance
- **Friend pendant** - shows demand but failed on trust and value delivery
- **Limitless pendant** - validates professional use case with consent-forward approach
- **Humane AI Pin** - cautionary tale of "future tech" without daily value loop

**Lesson:** The hardware pattern works. Success depends on trust, clear value, and daily utility.

## Technology Advantage: Voice-First Understanding

**Why now?** Voice understanding is moving beyond speech-to-text + LLM.

**Modulate ELM (Ensemble Listening Model):**
- Voice-first intent understanding combining words + acoustic signals
- Captures prosody, timing, tone, emotion - the "how it was said" layer
- More accurate and cost-effective than general foundation models

**Tizzy's moat is NOT just Modulate:**
1. Autism-specific dataset (consented, labeled, proprietary)
2. Autism-first coaching product design
3. Personalization layer (your norm vs population norm)
4. Distribution channel trust (therapy, clinics, institutions)
5. Governance and privacy posture

## Business Model

**Impact-first, sustainably:**
- Hardware at cost or subsidized
- Subscription for coaching service ($20-40/month)
- Clinic/therapist licensing for dashboards
- Scholarship pool for low-income access
- **Never sell user data to third parties**

## 30-Day Value Promise

Within 30 days of use:
- Identify user's highest-impact conversational patterns
- Show where and when they occur
- Provide coaching plan improving 1-2 target skills measurably

Examples: Fewer breakdowns, better repair, improved reciprocity, reduced masking burnout

## Key Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| **Social acceptance** | Build legitimacy through assistive tech positioning, consent-forward design |
| **Privacy/surveillance concerns** | Transparent data handling, local processing, auditable trust model |
| **COPPA/legal (minors)** | Start with 18+ adults, build compliance before expanding to minors |
| **Accuracy/harm** | Uncertainty handling, personalization, avoid teaching masking |
| **Hardware audio quality** | Dual-mic array, robust signal processing, form factor testing |

## Execution Timeline

**0-90 days:** Prototype hardware, capture pipeline, app MVP, recruit 20-50 adult beta users
**3-6 months:** Prove 30-day improvement metrics, build therapist export, privacy dashboard
**6-12 months:** Scale dataset ethically, expand coaching, institutional partnerships
**12-24 months:** School expansion only after compliance validated

5. **Clinical alignment:** Built on ASHA frameworks, therapy-friendly
6. **Timing:** Category accelerating, tech mature, market ready
