- Perplexity should be used for all **social, intellectual, and financial**
  health tasks. Amber complements with **spiritual, emotional, and
  physical** health. Perplexity is deeply embedded in our product; the
  two work hand in hand.
- I am thinking about things on a hundred-year timeline — when people
  are on different planets or in barren lands. They should be able to
  create, live authentic lives, and be fully present with the technology
  we are building.

  but are not currently understood fully because the English language

  creating novel, unique data of how the world is choosing to pray and
  live "healthy lives" that should be interpreted by our models.
- Study Blue Modern Advisory and Rytual Health and you will see how we
  are building communities that have the same mirroring that Perplexity
  and Amber should have. This is my life's mission to modernize Vedic
  thought; it is my way of externalizing my faith (Caleb's too — he's
  a Christian).
- The agents doing all the work on both ends need to be seamless,
  spiritually aligned, and real. They need to move faster yet with more
  spiritual conviction than any other startup.
- Perplexity is where knowledge begins. Amber is where connection begins.
