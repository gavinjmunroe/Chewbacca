The Christian consumer wedge is Chewbacca's first consumer play: Bible verses
that pile up on the HUD every 15 min (bin/hud-verse), and a double-click that
opens the HUD chat to talk abt the verse (Screen `ask=`,
[[reference_hud_card_ask]]).

Caleb, 2026-10-16 6:12pm: "I think this is a feature many christians would
use and could highk be a wedge for chewbs". At 6:27pm: "this could be such a
fire consumer wedge for chewbs to start with in parallel with the GTME stuff".

**How to apply:** it runs ALONGSIDE the GTME client work and never takes
time from it. The plan is the work ledger's cw-0 to cw-7 (teardown of
YouVersion/Hallow/Glorify, interviews, pre-mortem, channels, spec, a 7-day
pilot w 5 GenCollege/ACTS testers, then one decision page). Findings go in
docs/plans/CHRISTIAN-WEDGE.md. Falsifier: if 5 testers aren't still opening
verses on day 7, it isn't a wedge. Verse lists must become per-user (his is
KJV, victory and contentment); ESV and NIV need permission to ship.
