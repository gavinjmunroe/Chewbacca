\begin{abstract}
We formalize \textsc{tiwari-enrich} as a Dempster--Shafer
evidence-combination system over a frame of six dimensions of health.
Each source of evidence about a person $p$ --- web-scraped
\textsc{togari} records, private \textsc{amber-id} snippets from four
ingestion pipes, and \textsc{newton} heuristics --- contributes a
basic probability assignment over the power set of dimensions. We
combine them with a discounting operator that respects pipe trust so
that first-person check-ins (trust $=1.00$) dominate scraped LinkedIn
bios (trust $\approx 0.65$), and we never overwrite user-authored data
with externally scraped data. We give closed-form update rules and a
decision rule for downstream consumers.
\end{abstract}

\section{Frame of Discernment}

Let
\[
\Theta = \{\theta_{\text{spi}}, \theta_{\text{emo}}, \theta_{\text{phy}},
            \theta_{\text{int}}, \theta_{\text{soc}}, \theta_{\text{fin}}\}
\]
be the six-element frame. A \emph{basic probability assignment} (BPA)
is a function $m:2^\Theta\to[0,1]$ with $m(\emptyset)=0$ and
$\sum_{A\subseteq\Theta} m(A)=1$. The value $m(A)$ is the evidence
committed to ``the proposition that person $p$ is relevant to
dimensions $A$ (but not narrower).''

A singleton $m(\{\theta_{\text{phy}}\})$ says ``the evidence supports
physical health only.'' A mass on $\Theta$ itself is pure ignorance
and corresponds to a vector with $\texttt{health\_dimension}="*"$.

\section{Evidence Sources}

For each person $p$ and query context $q$, we have up to three
sources of evidence:
\begin{itemize}
\item $\mathcal{S}_T$: \textsc{togari} returns $k_T$ records, each
      with a dominant dimension and a source-reliability score. Pipe
      trust $\tau_T$ is fixed at the scraper's historical precision
      (default $0.65$).
\item $\mathcal{S}_A$: \textsc{amber-id} returns $k_A$ Pinecone
      matches; each carries $\texttt{health\_dimension}$ and a per-vector
      trust $\tau_i$ from its ingest pipe
      ($\texttt{agent}=0.65,\;\texttt{health}=0.95,\;\texttt{checkin}=1.00,\;\texttt{relationship}=0.50$).
\item $\mathcal{S}_N$: \textsc{newton} emits heuristic boosts (e.g.
      ``we iMessage this person mostly about workouts'').
\end{itemize}

From source $s$ and record $i$, we construct a singleton-supported BPA
\begin{equation}
m_{s,i}(\{\theta_{d_i}\}) = \tau_i \cdot q_i,
\quad
m_{s,i}(\Theta) = 1 - \tau_i \cdot q_i,
\end{equation}
where $d_i$ is the record's declared health dimension and
$q_i\in[0,1]$ is its query relevance (cosine similarity for Pinecone,
matching score for Togari). The residual mass on $\Theta$ is the
``I don't know'' reserve that discounting produces.

Records with $\texttt{health\_dimension}="*"$ (cross-dimensional) put
all mass on $\Theta$ itself: they carry information but do not commit
to a dimension.

\section{Shafer's Discounting}

Each source is further discounted by its \emph{pipe-level} trust
$\tau_{\text{pipe}}$ via Shafer's discounting operator:
\begin{align}
m'(A) &= \tau_{\text{pipe}}\, m(A), \quad A \neq \Theta, \\
m'(\Theta) &= 1 - \tau_{\text{pipe}} \bigl(1 - m(\Theta)\bigr).
\end{align}
This preserves proportions within singletons while returning unused
belief to the total-ignorance cell. The relationship pipe (trust
$=0.50$) therefore contributes at most half its nominal mass to any
specific dimension.

\section{Dempster's Rule of Combination}

Given two discounted BPAs $m'_1, m'_2$, their combination is
\begin{equation}
(m'_1\oplus m'_2)(A)
 \;=\; \frac{1}{1-K}\sum_{B\cap C = A \neq \emptyset} m'_1(B)\, m'_2(C),
\end{equation}
with conflict
$K = \sum_{B\cap C=\emptyset} m'_1(B)\, m'_2(C)$.
Combination is associative and commutative, so we can fuse any number
of BPAs in any order.

\subsection{Monotone trust --- first-person data never gets overwritten}

\begin{theorem}[First-person dominance]
Let $m_u$ be a BPA from a check-in (pipe $\texttt{checkin}$,
$\tau_{\text{pipe}}=1.00$) with singleton mass
$m_u(\{\theta_d\}) = \alpha > 0$ and $m_u(\Theta) = 1-\alpha$. Let
$m_w$ be any BPA from a pipe with $\tau_{\text{pipe}} < 1$. Then
after discounting, $m'_w$ is absolutely continuous with respect to
$m_u$ on singletons: for every singleton $\{\theta\}$,
\[
m'_w(\{\theta\}) \le \tau_{\text{pipe}}(w) \cdot m_w(\{\theta\}) < m_w(\{\theta\}),
\]
and the combined belief
$(m_u\oplus m'_w)(\{\theta_d\}) \ge m_u(\{\theta_d\})$
whenever $\theta_d \in B$ for every positive-mass focal element $B$ of
$m'_w$.
\end{theorem}

The theorem formalizes the product rule: \emph{scraped data can confirm
a check-in but cannot override it.} Implementation fires a soft alarm
when a non-checkin source attempts to contradict a check-in on the
same $(p, d)$ pair.

\section{Belief and Plausibility for Downstream Consumers}

Given the fused BPA $m^\star$, the \emph{belief} and
\emph{plausibility} of a dimension $\theta_d$ are
\[
\mathrm{Bel}(\{\theta_d\}) = \sum_{B \subseteq \{\theta_d\}} m^\star(B) = m^\star(\{\theta_d\}),
\qquad
\mathrm{Pl}(\{\theta_d\}) = \sum_{B \cap \{\theta_d\} \neq \emptyset} m^\star(B).
\]
The iOS client displays $\mathrm{Bel}$ as ``confidence we know this
about them'' and $\mathrm{Pl} - \mathrm{Bel}$ as ``uncertainty we
still hold.''

The enrichment API returns, for each person $p$ and each dimension
$d$:
$\langle \mathrm{Bel}_d, \mathrm{Pl}_d, \text{top-3 citations sorted by mass} \rangle$.

\section{Decision Rule}

For downstream ranking, we collapse $m^\star$ to a probability vector
using the \emph{pignistic transform}:
\[
\mathrm{BetP}(\{\theta_d\}) = \sum_{B \ni \theta_d} \frac{m^\star(B)}{|B|}.
\]
This distributes mass on non-singleton focal elements uniformly across
their members and is the unique transform consistent with linear
utility under Shafer's framework.

\section{Why Dempster--Shafer and not Bayes}

A plain Bayesian update would force every record to commit to a
complete distribution over dimensions, which discards the information
carried by cross-dimensional ($\Theta$-mass) vectors. Dempster--Shafer
is strictly more expressive here: it separates
\emph{``I don't know''} (mass on $\Theta$) from
\emph{``I am uncertain among specific options''} (mass on a subset),
which is exactly the distinction amber-id's data model makes.

\section*{Implementation pointers}

\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{Tiwari/tiwari-enrich/index.js} constructs BPAs from
      the three sources and returns $\mathrm{Bel}$/$\mathrm{Pl}$ per
      dimension.
\item \texttt{Tiwari/tiwari-enrich/src/ds.js} implements discounting,
      Dempster's rule, and the pignistic transform.
\item The $\tau_{\text{pipe}}$ values are the
      \texttt{PIPE\_TRUST} map in
      \texttt{amber-id/src/lib/dimensions.js}.
\end{itemize}
