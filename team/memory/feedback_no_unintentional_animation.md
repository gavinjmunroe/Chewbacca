On every site, the figure moves and the text holds. Text that changes in
place swaps or crossfades without moving; it never replays an entrance keyed to
state, and a page carries one rise or fade-up entrance at most.
`site-gate research` fails a keyed entrance and three or more rises in one
component.

**Why:** 2026-10-10, Erik Fish's map caption was keyed to replay a fade-up on
every scroll step and four scenes slid their lines up one after another.
Caleb: "upfade upfade upfade bro it hurts my eyes", then "Make sure chewb NEVER
makes annoying unintentional animations".

**How to apply:** before adding motion, name what it shows the reader. If the
answer is "it looks smooth", cut it. Related: [[feedback_no_hard_cutoffs]],
[[feedback_no_walls_of_text]], [[feedback_website_four_layers]].
