What you get

Four properties that distinguish Togari from a generic enrichment vendor.

One person, every source

Email, LinkedIn, phone, full name. Match priority is deterministic and the confidence score is on every record.

Real people only

A heuristic + Claude Haiku classifier rejects organizations, role accounts, and bots. Every contact carries a realness score.

Private per user

Your contacts are scoped to your Auth0 identity. Other BMA accounts cannot read or match against your pool. Ever.

Apple Contacts source-of-truth

Bidirectional CardDAV sync with your iCloud address book. New contacts created in Togari land in Apple Contacts within 30 seconds.

How it works

Three steps. Five minutes.

  1. STEP 01

    Connect a platform

    LinkedIn is the fastest start. One click, no DevTools, no copy-paste of cookies.

  2. STEP 02

    Generate an API key

    A long-lived tgi_live_ token, scoped to you, revocable anytime.

  3. STEP 03

    Paste into Clay

    One HTTP API column. Match by name, email, LinkedIn, or phone. Result is back in under a second.
