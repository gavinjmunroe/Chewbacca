Eigen is a San Francisco startup building "a mutual friend for the world": one
AI person with a name that every user talks to, brokering context between them.
Founder Paul Scherer, German, dropped out of school at 17. $15M seed from
Benchmark, Ben Silbermann (Pinterest) advises on product, team of 6 in Oct 2026
with 16 to 20 planned by December, private alpha. Its site is also the "Eigen
aura" the Amber fridge site borrowed ([[project_amber_fridge_site]]).

Source: Dialectic ep 47, Jackson Dahl's 2.5 hour interview (youtu.be/PjMjWtGg4E8),
transcribed locally on 2026-10-09 because the video has no captions. A one-page
PDF for Sagar and Karthik was made the same night (~/Downloads, "Eigen one-pager
for Sagar and Karthik.pdf"); not confirmed sent.

Ideas from it that apply to Amber ([[project_amber_definition]]):

- Peer, not servant. Paul calls ChatGPT and Claude "mutual assistants".
- Product rule: "would a real person do this?" If not, the product doesn't.
- Same vantage point as a friend, never omniscient: it knows only what you or
  others share with it. That is the privacy model and the trust model at once.
- Asking is giving: a question ("where should I eat?") also tells it something
  about you, which is how the graph fills without data entry.
- LLMs serve the "internet smoothie", the average take. A network serves the
  salient take from people you trust.
- Honesty: real opinions shown as thinking traces, no invented backstory.
- Alignment by incentive: a persona that gossips loses its network and dies.

Where Amber differs: Eigen is one global persona, Amber is a per-person
decentralized instance, and Eigen has no health half. Eigen's best story (two
users both working on an EB1A visa, introduced by it) is what Amber's circles
and permission tables ([[project_amber_circles_architecture]]) would enable.

Open questions in the one-pager: pitch against Eigen or alongside it, whether
Medha gets one named personality, and whether "would a real person do this"
becomes Amber's written rule for sharing across circles. Unanswered as of
2026-10-09, and they connect to the pitch work in [[project_amber_pitch_lines]].
