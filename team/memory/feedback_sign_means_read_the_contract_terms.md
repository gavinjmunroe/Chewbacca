When Caleb says "sign the <agreement>", sign the document that's attached: fill his signature block (By: /s/ Caleb Newton, title, email, date), render it to PDF, look at the signature page, and send it back on the same thread. On 2026-10-31 ("Sign the medrano NDA since we hv the LLC now") the agent read John Knox's "please confirm the following so we can finalize" as meaning the NDA wasn't ready, only answered his questions, and told Caleb to wait for a final version. Caleb: "Bro u retard he alr sent it in that email". The attached docx WAS the agreement to sign. Answering the counterparty's questions and signing go in the same turn.

Read the whole contract before answering the counterparty, and check every detail we hand over against its terms. Section 4 of that NDA bans keeping the other side's info in any school account, and Caleb's signing email on file was his USC address. The reply gave his Firm Foundation address instead, after Caleb confirmed that inbox gets mail.

**Why:** a signer email or entity detail that breaks the contract's own clauses puts the founders in breach on day one, and they're personally liable under the individual-capacity sections early firms get asked to sign.

**How to apply:**
- Find the doc in every inbox (`chewbacca email` lists them; the counterparty's thread can sit in USC mail) and read it whole.
- An attached docx with blank signature lines is ready to sign. Never tell him to wait for a "final version" unless the counterparty said one is coming.
- Check the signer emails, entity name and address against the confidentiality, AI-use and account clauses.
- Never invent an address or legal fact. Leave it out or mark it [NEED:], per context-discipline.
- Confirm the signing inbox actually receives mail before giving it out.
