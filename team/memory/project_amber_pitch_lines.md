Captured 2026-09-01. Positioning language being used verbally when explaining
Amber to tech people. Attribution not confirmed, the "I'd say moreSo" reads as
one person correcting another, likely Sagar and Caleb or Sagar and Shirley.

The three lines:

1. "Obsidian X Snapchat X Clay X Contacts app"
2. "Obsidian + Wispr x Salesforce x Clay, I'd say moreSo"
3. "It's Perplexity for People. The world's first AI awareness engine."

**Line 3 is the keeper.** One referent, not four. It has real provenance: Amber
was born in Perplexity's Billion Dollar Business competition in May 2026, so the
comparison is origin story rather than borrowed shine. And "Perplexity for
People" correctly promises _answers about people_, not _a database of people_ , 
which is the actual product. Drop "the world's first AI awareness engine," or at
least "world's first": unfalsifiable category claim, and importance puffery by
Caleb's own writing rules. "AI awareness engine" alone is fine and "awareness"
is already load-bearing in the codebase (branch `feat/medha-vision-awareness`).

**Line 2 is a regression from line 1**, against Sagar's own doctrine. Swapping
Snapchat for Salesforce makes the pitch more CRM-shaped, and
[[project_amber_definition]] records him saying the opposite, repeatedly: "Every
CRM ever built is the corporate version of that same problem, badly solved... If
a feature makes Amber more CRM-shaped, it is going the wrong direction." Line 2
now names _two_ GTM tools (Salesforce and Clay) and no personal one.

What each referent is actually doing:

- **Obsidian**, right, and the strongest of the four. Own your data, local
  graph, links between notes. Matches "decentralized" and "common save file."
- **Wispr**, right, and better than most people would guess. Voice capture is
  genuinely core: `amber-ios` ships `VoiceNoteRecorder`, `VoiceNotePlayer`,
  `Waveform`, and a draggable recorder egg. This is the real input method.
- **Snapchat**, underrated, and dropping it was a mistake. It encodes personal
  not professional, people you actually keep up with, lightweight and frequent.
  It is the anti-CRM referent in the list.
- **Clay / Salesforce**, the liability. Both say pipeline. Both invite the exact
  misread the doctrine exists to prevent.

**The biggest problem is what all three lines omit: health.** The canonical
one-sentence definition is "a decentralized, secure instance of your most
important assets: **your health and your people**." Every pitch line above keeps
"your people" and drops "your health" entirely. That is half the sentence gone,
and health is the half that makes Amber not-a-CRM and the half Rytual Health
exists to build. Worth raising directly: is health being deprioritized, or is
the pitch just drifting?
