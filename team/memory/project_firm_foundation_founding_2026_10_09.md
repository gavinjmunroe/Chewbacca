Firm Foundation is Caleb and Gavin's 50/50 AI implementation agency for churches. Plan repo: github.com/gavinjmunroe/firm-foundation (DECISIONS.md is the source of truth). What was said on the 2026-10-09 founding call:

- Name: Cornerstone, Agape, Cross Intelligence and churchsolutions.ai were all considered, then dropped. Gavin bought **firmfoundation.tech** on Porkbun (~$7 first year). Cornerstone might survive as the name of one service.
- Site: Gavin builds it with full creative freedom. Caleb's only bar is "cooler than the Lemma site" (replica at calebnewtonusc/lemma-replica, Gavin has access). Direction is the Lemma look mixed with the warmth of Upper Room's site, in blue and white. Faith shows up tastefully: "believers building for the church", one verse, mission statement at the bottom. Logo idea: a cross woven out of circuit-board traces, animated on landing. Services copy can come from the USCTTS site.
- Pricing was discussed, not settled. First month free. The first ~10 churches get an early-adopter rate around $5K, rising toward $50K later. Gavin thinks $10K/mo is too low. Upsells floated: AEO through Profound (~$1K), a chatbot on the church's site, site redesigns, a church CRM to replace Google Sheets, and HUD-based onboarding. AI UGC creators were shot down because church marketing is won on search/SEO.
- Rules: never move money, no AI pastoral care, data isn't sold, we host, weekdays only.
- Leads: a warm intro to Upper Room (Dallas) through Chase Durkin, the young-adults pastor whose site Gavin built. For outreach, Clay to churches of 250+ people, starting in the US.
- Closers: Gavin's friend Finn on commission, or BMA's two new salespeople at ~20%.
- Competitors looked at: Tithely (53K churches, people/check-in suite) and ministryautomation.com (low-effort AI site).
- Split of work: Gavin builds the site, Caleb plans Clay strategy with Karthik. Sagar said he supports it as side work. Sunday 10/11 sync settles the open decisions, including which entity to use: an existing LLC with a DBA, or a new Texas LLC with a one-page 50/50 operating agreement.
