## Executive Summary

Tizzy's business model is designed for **impact-first sustainability**, not hypergrowth extraction. The core thesis: **hardware at cost + subscription for coaching + clinical licensing = recurring revenue that funds the mission.** This document outlines revenue streams, unit economics, cost structure, funding requirements, and a realistic path to profitability.

**Key Financial Targets:**
- **Year 1:** $125K revenue (proof of concept)
- **Year 2:** $1.4M ARR (product-market fit)
- **Year 3:** $4M+ ARR (path to profitability)
- **Gross margin target:** 60-70% (subscription-driven)
- **Break-even:** Month 30-36 post-launch

## Revenue Model: Three Pillars

### Pillar 1: Consumer Subscription (Primary Revenue)

**Product:** Tizzy app + coaching service
**Customer:** Parents of autistic children, autistic adults
**Pricing:** $29-49/month (or $290-490/year prepaid with discount)

**What's Included:**
- Real-time haptic coaching
- Post-conversation review and insights
- Weekly progress dashboards
- Cloud sync and data retention (30-90 days)
- Monthly coaching reports
- Optional therapist sharing

**Why Subscription, Not One-Time:**
- Coaching improves over time (personalization, model updates)
- Ongoing service costs (cloud compute, storage, support)
- Recurring revenue funds R&D and dataset collection
- Aligns incentives (we only win if users see long-term value)

**Target Subscription Metrics:**
- Trial-to-paid conversion: 40-50%
- Monthly churn: 5-7% (best in class for health/wellness)
- LTV (customer lifetime value): $500-800
- Median subscription duration: 12-18 months

**Subscription Tiers (Potential):**

| Tier | Price/Month | Features |
|------|-------------|----------|
| **Basic** | $29 | Haptic coaching, weekly reports, 30-day retention |
| **Premium** | $49 | Daily insights, 90-day retention, therapist sharing, priority support |
| **Family** | $79 | 2-3 devices, shared dashboard, family progress tracking |

---

### Pillar 2: Hardware Sales (Near Cost, Strategic)

**Product:** Tizzy necklace (or future form factors)
**Customer:** Same as subscription (bundled or separate)
**Pricing:** $299-399 one-time

**Hardware Economics:**
- **Bill of materials (BOM):** $80-120 (microphones, battery, haptics, BLE chip, enclosure)
- **Manufacturing cost:** $150-200 (assembly, QA, packaging, logistics)
- **Gross margin:** 30-50% initially, improves with volume

**Why Near Cost:**
- Hardware is the hook, subscription is the business
- Lowers barrier to trial (vs. $500-700 premium wearable)
- Reduces risk for parents ("What if my kid won't wear it?")

**Hardware Subsidy Strategy:**
- Early pilots: Free device loan (to validate product-market fit)
- Data donors: $100-200 discount (incentivize dataset growth)
- Therapy channel: Near-cost pricing (clinics bulk purchase)
- Institutions: Bundled into annual license (school districts, universities)

**Volume Targets:**
- Year 1: 350 devices shipped (mostly subsidized)
- Year 2: 1,500 devices
- Year 3: 3,000 devices

---

### Pillar 3: Clinical & Institutional Licensing (B2B)

**Product:** Therapist dashboard + cohort analytics + training
**Customer:** Speech-language pathologists, autism clinics, school districts, universities
**Pricing:** $500-1,000/year per therapist OR $100-150/year per student (institutional)

**What's Included:**
- Dashboard access to patient/student insights (with consent)
- Cohort-level analytics (trends, outcomes, benchmarks)
- Training and certification (CEU-eligible for SLPs)
- Priority support and customization
- Co-marketing and research collaboration

**Why B2B Licensing:**
- Validates clinical credibility (essential for parent trust)
- High-margin revenue (minimal incremental cost)
- Distribution channel (therapists recommend Tizzy to clients)
- Dataset growth (therapy partnerships = consented data)

**B2B Revenue Targets:**
- Year 1: 10 clinic partnerships × $1,000/year = $10K
- Year 2: 50 therapists × $1,000 + 500 institutional students × $120 = $110K
- Year 3: 150 therapists × $1,000 + 2,000 institutional students × $120 = $390K

## Unit Economics: Consumer Subscription

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

**Blended CAC Target:** $150-250 per customer

**Breakdown by Channel:**

| Channel | CAC | Conversion Rate | Notes |
|---------|-----|-----------------|-------|
| Therapy Referral | $50-100 | 30-40% | Warm leads, high trust |
| Word-of-Mouth | $0-50 | 20-30% | Organic, referral incentives |
| Content Marketing | $100-150 | 5-10% | SEO, blogs, YouTube |
| Paid Ads (Facebook/Google) | $200-300 | 2-5% | Cold traffic, testing |
| Conferences/Events | $150-200 | 10-15% | SLP conferences, autism events |

**CAC Calculation Example (Year 2):**
- Total marketing spend: $300K
- New customers acquired: 1,500
- Blended CAC: $200

---

### Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)

**LTV Target:** $500-800

**Calculation:**
- Average subscription price: $40/month
- Average subscription duration: 12-18 months (median)
- Gross margin: 70% (post-hardware sale)
- **LTV = $40 × 15 months × 70% = $420** (conservative)
- **LTV = $40 × 18 months × 75% = $540** (realistic)
- **LTV = $40 × 24 months × 80% = $768** (best case)

**Hardware Contribution:**
- Hardware gross profit: $100-150 per device
- **Total LTV (hardware + subscription) = $540 + $125 = $665**

**LTV:CAC Ratio:**
- Conservative: $665 / $200 = **3.3:1** (healthy, sustainable)
- Target: $800 / $150 = **5.3:1** (best-in-class)

**Payback Period:**
- CAC $200, subscription $40/month, 70% margin
- Payback = $200 / ($40 × 70%) = **7 months** (excellent)

---

### Churn Analysis

**Target Monthly Churn:** 5-7%

**Churn Benchmarks (Comparable Products):**
- Fitness apps: 10-15% monthly churn
- Health coaching: 6-8%
- Therapy/mental health: 5-7%
- Assistive tech (hearing aids, CGM): 3-5%

**Tizzy Churn Hypothesis:**
- **High churn risk:** First 90 days (if no value perceived)
- **Low churn risk:** Post-6 months (habit formed, outcomes proven)

**Churn Reduction Strategies:**
- 30-day onboarding program (daily tips, progress tracking)
- Weekly "wins" notifications (celebrate improvements)
- Monthly personalized reports (share with therapist or parent)
- Community engagement (user forums, success stories)
- Proactive support (reach out if usage drops)

**Churn Cohort Projection:**

| Cohort Month | Retained Users | Churn Rate |
|--------------|----------------|------------|
| Month 1 | 100% | 0% (trial) |
| Month 2 | 85% | 15% (trial-to-paid drop) |
| Month 3 | 75% | 12% |
| Month 4 | 68% | 9% |
| Month 6 | 60% | 7% |
| Month 12 | 50% | 5% (stabilized) |

## Cost Structure: Building with Discipline

### Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)

**Hardware COGS (per unit):**
- Bill of materials: $100
- Manufacturing & assembly: $60
- Packaging & logistics: $15
- Quality assurance: $10
- Warranty reserve: $15
- **Total Hardware COGS: $200**

**Subscription COGS (per user/month):**
- Cloud compute (inference, storage): $5
- Modulate API fees: $3 (estimated, TBD based on contract)
- Data transfer & bandwidth: $1
- Support & success: $2 (amortized)
- **Total Subscription COGS: $11/month**

**Gross Margin:**
- Hardware: ($300 - $200) / $300 = **33%**
- Subscription: ($40 - $11) / $40 = **73%**
- **Blended Gross Margin (Year 2): ~60-65%**

## Path to Profitability

### Break-Even Analysis

**Assumptions:**
- Blended gross margin: 65%
- Fixed OpEx: $150K/month (Year 3 run rate)
- Target: Gross profit covers OpEx

**Break-Even Calculation:**
- Monthly OpEx: $150K
- Required gross profit: $150K
- Required revenue: $150K / 65% = **$231K/month = $2.8M/year**

**Target: Break-even by Month 30-36 (mid-Year 3)**

## Business Model Risks & Mitigation

### Risk: Hardware Manufacturing Delays

**Impact:** Product launch delays, revenue miss, user frustration
**Mitigation:**
- Start with dev kit form factor (faster to market)
- Partner with experienced CM (contract manufacturer)
- Build 3-6 month buffer inventory
- Transparent communication with early adopters

---

### Risk: Low Subscription Retention

**Impact:** LTV collapses, CAC payback extends, profitability delayed
**Mitigation:**
- Obsess over 30-day onboarding (prove value fast)
- Weekly engagement loops (progress reports, wins)
- Proactive support (usage drop alerts)
- Long-term outcome tracking (6-12 month check-ins)

---

### Risk: Modulate Dependency

**Impact:** Pricing increase, service disruption, loss of leverage
**Mitigation:**
- Negotiate favorable multi-year contract
- Build proprietary coaching layer (not just Modulate wrapper)
- Invest in backup models (Whisper + fine-tuning)
- Exit strategy in contract (switch costs minimized)

---

### Risk: Privacy Breach or Trust Crisis

**Impact:** User exodus, regulatory fines, brand death
**Mitigation:**
- Privacy-first architecture from day one
- Third-party security audits (SOC 2, HIPAA-readiness)
- Transparent governance (public data dashboard)
- Incident response plan (communication, remediation)

---

### Risk: Clinical Credibility Failure

**Impact:** Therapists don't recommend, parents don't trust, sales stall
**Mitigation:**
- Partner with respected SLPs and autism clinics early
- Publish case studies and outcome data
- Ground coaching in ASHA benchmarks and DSM-5
- Avoid overclaiming (coaching, not cure)

---

### Risk: Market Timing (Too Early)

**Impact:** Users not ready for always-on wearable, adoption slow
**Mitigation:**
- Lead with pain point (not futuristic tech hype)
- Focus on transitions (college, new school) where pain is acute
- Therapy channel provides validation and distribution
- Patient capital (expect 3-5 year path to profitability)

## Conclusion: Impact-First, Sustainably

Tizzy's business model is designed to **solve a real problem for a underserved community, sustainably.** This is not a hypergrowth moonshot. This is disciplined, mission-driven company building:

- **Revenue is recurring** (subscription + licensing)
- **Margins improve over time** (hardware volume, cloud efficiency)
- **CAC is manageable** (therapy channel, word-of-mouth)
- **LTV is strong** (high retention, long-term value)
- **Path to profitability is realistic** (Year 3-4)

The business model funds the mission. The mission attracts the users. The users generate the dataset. The dataset builds the moat. **That's how Tizzy becomes a generational assistive technology company.**
