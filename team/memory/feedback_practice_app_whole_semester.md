A course practice app (the Railway study apps Chewbacca builds) covers the whole semester so far, chapter by chapter, with the nearest deadline first.

**Why:** On 2026-10-11 the BISC 101 app was built for Midterm 2 only. The student's reply: "put the entire bio semester in there I know none of it". Ch 1 to 5 then went in as 13 drills (drawn atoms, tap-the-pH scale, bonds, water, molecule sorts, organelles, fact decks), all pulled word for word from Lectures 1 to 9 and Labs 1 to 3.

**How to apply:** Pull every posted deck (pptx XML, binary .ppt TextCharsAtom 0x0FA0 and TextBytesAtom 0x0FA8, lab PDFs) before writing items, and cite the slide on each one. Final audit opens every screen on the live URL at 1440 and 390 in Chromium and WebKit, checking errors, blank screens and scrollWidth. Per-drill tests missed a planner row 58px too wide that this audit caught. Rule lives in skills/coursework/SKILL.md. Related: [[feedback_practice_tool_is_a_session]].
