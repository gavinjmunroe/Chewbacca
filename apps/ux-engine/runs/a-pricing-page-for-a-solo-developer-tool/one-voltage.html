Questions

Can I change plans later?
Any time. Upgrades apply immediately with prorated credit. Downgrades take effect at the next billing date.
What happens when I hit the storage limit?
Your projects stay live. You'll get an email and 14 days to upgrade or clean up before anything pauses.
Do you offer refunds?
Within 7 days of a new billing period, if you haven't used the paid features. No questions.
Is there a student discount?
Email us from a .edu address. Pro is half price while you're enrolled. Hobby stays free regardless.
Do you have an API?
Yes, on Pro and Studio. Full documentation at devkit.sh/docs/api. Rate limits scale with your plan.
