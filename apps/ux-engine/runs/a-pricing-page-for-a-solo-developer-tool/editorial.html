Common questions

What happens to my data if I cancel Pro?

Your local data stays local — nothing is deleted. Sync pauses. You have 90 days to export anything stored in the cloud before it is removed.

Can I use this on a machine I don't own?

The free tier works anywhere without an account. Pro requires a login on shared or employer machines. Data stays encrypted and tied to your account, not the machine.

Is the core open source?

The CLI is MIT licensed and on GitHub. The sync infrastructure and web dashboard are closed. Contributions to the core are welcome.

How does Team billing work?

One invoice per month for the number of active seats. Adding a seat mid-month is prorated. There is no minimum seat count and no annual commitment required.

Can I switch plans mid-year?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the next billing date.

Do you offer refunds?

Annual plans are non-refundable. Monthly plans can be cancelled before the next billing date for a full credit toward a future purchase.
