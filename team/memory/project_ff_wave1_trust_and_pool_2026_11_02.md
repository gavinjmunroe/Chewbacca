Firm Foundation's wave 1 plan is ops/WAVE-1.md in calebnewtonusc/firm-foundation
(commit 9ac819a, 2026-11-02). It came from Caleb asking for "the wisest possible
gtm strategy" for the first wave.

What was measured, so nobody re-derives it:

- Jev (jev-1.13.0) read six email 1 versions as a skeptical executive pastor.
  Every cold version read as spam at 0.77 to 0.80. Wording barely moved that.
  "No call needed" cut pushiness from 0.69 to ~0.4, and naming Andrew Laffoon
  plus firmfoundation.tech/team lifted trust from 0.47 to 0.58. Reruns stayed
  within 0.1. Jev can't score warmth, so warm intros are a separate track.
- Jev rated the "first-time guests, plan a visit" framing's fit at 0.29 to
  0.37 for Catholic and mainline churches and 0.55 to 0.71 for evangelical ones.
  Office administrators scored 0.25 to 0.27 as website deciders.
- Evangelical churches in band with a named buyer AND an email on their own
  site: 22 SoCal, 29 DFW. Another 68 are named but have no email, and 343 are
  role inbox only. At the 5/10 a day ramp SoCal runs out by about 11/11.

**Why:** the 10/11 lists ranked by site pain and ignored tradition and title,
so 9 of the first 15 SoCal sends were Catholic, mainline or an office admin.

**How to apply:** for any FF list or copy change, start from WAVE-1.md's gates
(evangelical, 2+ services, buyer title, failing guest path) and ranked lists in
ops/wave1/. No new first emails 11/25 to 1/4 (Advent). The falsifier: 10 warm
asks + ~60 cold to named evangelical buyers with zero conversations by 11/20
means the free fix list isn't the opener.
