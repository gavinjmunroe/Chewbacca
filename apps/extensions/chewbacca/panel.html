Let Chewbacca read this site?

Chewbacca reads the page you picked and puts it in your chat. It reads that site only, and only when you ask.

Chewbacca

Your chat tab, with your classes, calendar and files behind it.

Checking for the Mac half...

Free, with nothing installed

  1. Summarize a tab

    Open the Chewbacca pill in ChatGPT or Claude and pick a tab, or right-click any page.

  2. What's due

    Signed in to Brightspace in another tab? The pill pulls the next two weeks into your chat.

  3. Draft a reply

    Select a message on any page, right-click, Draft a reply with Chewbacca.

Get the Mac half

  1. Put Chewbacca on your Mac

    Copy this line, open Terminal (press Command and Space, type Terminal, press Return), paste it and press Return. It takes about ten minutes.

    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/calebnewtonusc/Chewbacca/main/start.sh | bash

  2. Connect this browser

    The install does this. If Chewbacca was already on your Mac, run chewbacca browser install once.

  3. Let it see your stuff

    Each button opens the right switch in System Settings. Turn on Chewbacca's tools there.

  4. Teach your chat

    Back in ChatGPT or Claude, open the Chewbacca pill and press Teach this chat the tools. Reads run right away. Anything that writes asks you in a box on your Mac first, never in the page.
