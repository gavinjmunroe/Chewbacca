Caleb, 2026-10-11, after chewb reported ACAD 185 had "0 files" on Brightspace
and asked where the slides were: "if you actually read brightspace you'd see a
masterspreadsheet with all the slides on it."

ACAD 185 has an empty content tree. Its home page links an announcement titled
"ACAD 185 Master Spreadsheet", and that Google Sheet's "Schedule and
Deliverables" tab links every lecture deck as Google Slides. Exported with
`/export/pdf` and `/export?format=xlsx`, all 13 decks and the sheet downloaded
without a sign-in.

**Why:** professors put materials wherever they like: content modules,
announcements, a pinned spreadsheet, a course homepage widget. A file count of
zero means the pull looked in one place, not that the material isn't there.

**How to apply:** before telling a student a course has no material, read the
course announcements (`/le/1.67/<ou>/news/`) and the homepage links for Google
Docs, Sheets and Slides, follow them, and export what they link. Never ask the
student where the slides are until that's done. Related:
[[feedback_usc_signin_uses_school_profile]], [[project_chewbacca_usc_stack_wedge]].
