Ghost is Zain Javaid's personal AI computer company (Core, a $3,499 home box,
a16z-led seed), already tracked as a Chewbacca competitor in
[[project_chewbacca_os]]. Caleb sent a behind-the-scenes video of their
pre-launch week (https://youtu.be/GqSXkWbYVZo) on 2026-10-07 and said "save wtv
we should so when it comes time for growth we go ham."

The full seven-point playbook lives in the Chewbacca repo, docs/COMPETITORS.md,
section "How Ghost runs its launch" (commit 4a28caa1 on main). Short version:
founder story first, weekly factory tours so buyers see the build, channel order
of events, then founder X, then PR and YouTube, a content plan for the dip after
launch written before launch, one moral belief carrying the brand ("I want to own
it"), concrete physical demos a stranger can check, and saying the pre-order
backlog out loud.

**How to apply:** when growth work on Chewbacca or Kyber starts, open that
section first and map each point onto a private note and
[[project_chewbacca_growth_engineer_direction]]. The privacy line can't be
borrowed until reasoning stops leaving the Mac. Nothing in the video was checked
independently; the $10M vs $11M seed figure differs between the video and
TechCrunch.
