A pitch for Anchored (or any venture) opens on the end state and its size, then why incumbents can't reach it, then the route from the wedge, then what's built, then the ask. The wedge alone sounds small.

**Why:** 2026-10-29, the Meridian demo-day video beats opened on "a text number that keeps churches from losing people". Caleb: "WHY ARE WE PITCHING SOMETHING SO SMALL WE NEED TO PITCH THE ROUTE TO TRILLION DOLLAR IDEA". ANCHORED-APP-SPEC.md in the anchored repo already said vision first, and it wasn't read before writing.

**How to apply:** read the venture's app spec before any pitch line. Anchored's: "the operating system for Christian life", 2.3B Christians (Pew 2025), Pushpay/Subsplash/Gloo sell to the church office and none own the member, four steps (church wedge, church as distribution, member identity, ecosystem like Shopify). Unsourced sizes ("trillion") stay out. Rule line in .claude/rules/deliverables.md.
