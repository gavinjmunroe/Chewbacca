# 6 Dimensions of Health

The foundational framework for all of Amber's architecture.

## The 6 Dimensions
| Dimension | Type | What It Tracks |
|---|---|---|
| Social | Masculine | Connections, relationships, community, belonging |
| Financial | Masculine | Stability, income, career momentum, abundance |
| Intellectual | Masculine | Learning, curiosity, growth, stimulation |
| Spiritual | Feminine | Faith, purpose, meaning, alignment with God |
| Physical | Feminine | Energy, sleep, movement, nutrition, fitness |
| Emotional | Feminine | Mood, regulation, peace, joy, overwhelm |

## Scoring
- Each dimension: 0-10 scale (10 = thriving)
- Inferred from conversations with confidence scores (0.6+ threshold)
- Self-reported via daily check-ins
- Rolling 7-day average for profiles

## How It's Used
- Every system prompt includes the health lens
- Contact profiles tagged with dimension-specific notes
- Calendar events classified by which dimension they serve
- Proactive messages calibrated to lowest dimension
- Group chat router responds to emotional distress signals
