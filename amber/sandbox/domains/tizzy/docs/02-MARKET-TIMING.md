# Market Timing: Why Now

## The Technology Inflection Point

The reason this product becomes possible now is that voice understanding is finally moving beyond "speech-to-text + LLM."

### What Traditional Systems Miss

Transcript-only systems lose huge parts of what matters in human conversation:

- **Prosody** - the melody and rhythm of speech
- **Timing** - pauses, hesitations, turn-taking speed
- **Tone** - emotional coloring and intensity
- **Interruptions** - overlap patterns and dominance
- **Hesitation** - uncertainty and processing signals
- **Escalation** - emotional volatility tracking
- **Sarcasm cues** - non-literal meaning signals
- **Emotional volatility** - mood tracking over time
- **The subtle "how it was said" layer** - paralinguistic features

## The Modulate Relationship Strategy

### How You Do Not Get Screwed

Structure the Modulate relationship like a vendor relationship with strict boundaries.

### Non-Negotiables for Tizzy

1. **Tizzy owns the autism dataset**
   - Modulate does not get training rights to it by default

2. **Inference-only license by default**
   - You pay for model usage
   - You do not gift them your moat

3. **No exclusivity that traps you**
   - If they demand exclusivity, it must be narrowly scoped
   - And expensive for them

4. **Auditability and change control**
   - If Modulate updates their model, you need regression testing
   - The ability to roll back for safety

5. **Exit plan**
   - You design your system so you can swap the underlying tone engine later

### What Modulate Brings vs. What You Build

| Component | Owner | Why |
|-----------|-------|-----|
| Voice intent understanding | Modulate (licensed) | They have the foundation model expertise |
| Prosody & emotion detection | Modulate (licensed) | Core to their ELM platform |
| Autism-specific pattern recognition | Tizzy (proprietary) | Your unique dataset and domain expertise |
| Coaching action mapping | Tizzy (proprietary) | Your product differentiation |
| Personalization engine | Tizzy (proprietary) | Your long-term value capture |
| Privacy & consent layer | Tizzy (proprietary) | Your trust moat |
