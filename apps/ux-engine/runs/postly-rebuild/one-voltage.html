Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Visual calendar

Drag, drop, and rearrange posts across your entire content calendar. See a full month at a glance without toggling between platforms.

Best-time scheduling

Queue reads your audience activity and automatically slots each post into the window that gets the most reach. Set it once, let it adapt.

Post analytics

Reach, engagement, and link clicks in one view. Know exactly what worked last week so you can schedule more like it this week.

Team collaboration

Assign posts for review, leave comments, and approve content before it goes live. Your whole team in one queue, nothing falling through the cracks.

Queue health score

A single number that tells you whether your posting frequency, content mix, and timing are on track. Green means go. Red means fix it now.

First-comment automation

Schedule hashtag blocks, CTA links, and follow-up replies to post automatically the moment each piece of content goes live.

How it works

Set it up once. Run it forever.

01

Connect your accounts

Link every platform in under two minutes. Queue handles the OAuth flow. Nothing else to configure. Every platform you add is live immediately.

02

Fill your queue

Write posts in bulk, import a spreadsheet, or paste a draft and Queue formats it for each platform automatically. One piece of content, every channel, one go.

03

Let it run

Queue posts on schedule, collects the results, and surfaces what to do more of. You show up for the creative work. It handles everything else.
