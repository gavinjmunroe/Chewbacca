2026-09-18. Caleb sent the Amber team his positioning for outbound. **The
framing is his, not generated**, and it settles several things that were open.

**The test that splits the two products:** are they at a laptop when they need
to manage their cognitive load? If yes, local Chewbacca already serves them and
there is no reason to sell hosted. If no, hosted is the only version that works
at all.

**Two Chewbacca ICPs (local, B2B, one-time contracts):**

- Partners at funds that just closed. Raising handed them a pile of founder
  contacts who are now their deal flow, they have the money, and the cognitive
  load is high.
- Founders of services firms 5 to 50 who just posted a business development
  role. That is the moment they are trying to outsource their own memory.
  **Chewbacca is cheaper than the hire**, so it sells as a one-time setup fee.

**One consumer ICP (hosted Amber, via MCP):** people who run events, clubs,
retreats and small groups. They meet new people weekly and live on their phone,
so hosted is the only thing that reaches them. Cut coaches and therapists
because they are on a laptop between sessions, and realtors because that is a
sales CRM pitch and Sagar has said Amber is not a CRM.

His line for it: **"AMBER IS FOR THE SHAMANS!!!"** which connects to the SHAMANS
assignment table Sagar ran in April 2026.

**Pricing:** Chewbacca sells one-time contracts. Amber starts on a free trial,
possibly freemium later.

**The migration thesis, which is the real strategic answer to "why would a local
user ever move":** today nobody would, and he says so plainly. Over time the
Amber ecosystem gets good enough and enough of a person's connections are
already in Amber that moving off local makes sense. **Until then the two
products target different people and should not be sold to the same person.**

He also told Sagar outbound should not start yet: Chewbacca has only ever run on
his machine, so he wants one to two more weeks iterating with Sagar and Karthik
plus friends on Claude subscriptions testing it, then a demo video attached to
the emails.
