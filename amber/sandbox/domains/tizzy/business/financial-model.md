# Financial Model: Revenue Projections & Unit Economics

**Document Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** February 13, 2026
**Owner:** Finance Team
**Planning Horizon:** 36 months (Years 1-3)

---

## Executive Summary

This financial model projects Tizzy's revenue, costs, and cash flow for the first three years of operation. The model is **conservative but achievable**, based on validated assumptions from comparable products, early pilot data, and realistic growth curves.

**Key Takeaways:**
- **Year 1 Revenue:** $189K (proof of concept, therapy channel launch)
- **Year 2 Revenue:** $1.04M (parent adoption ramp, product-market fit)
- **Year 3 Revenue:** $3.49M (institutional scale, approaching profitability)
- **Break-Even:** Month 30-36 (mid-Year 3)
- **Gross Margin:** 60-65% (subscription-driven model)
- **LTV:CAC Ratio:** 3.3:1 (healthy, sustainable unit economics)

---

## Revenue Model: Three Streams

### Stream 1: Consumer Subscriptions (Primary)

**Product:** Tizzy app + cloud coaching service
**Pricing:** $40/month average (tiered: $29 Basic, $49 Premium, $79 Family)
**Target Customers:** Autistic adults, parents of autistic children

**Assumptions:**
- Trial-to-paid conversion: 40-50%
- Monthly churn: 7% (Year 1), 6% (Year 2), 5% (Year 3)
- Average subscription duration: 12-18 months
- Upsell rate (Basic → Premium): 20% after 6 months

---

### Stream 2: Hardware Sales

**Product:** Tizzy necklace wearable
**Pricing:** $300 average (range: $299-399 depending on promo/bundle)
**Margin:** 33% gross margin (BOM $100 + manufacturing $60 + logistics/QA/warranty $40 = $200 COGS)

**Assumptions:**
- Hardware sold at near-cost (loss leader strategy)
- Subsidies for data donors ($100-200 discount)
- Volume discounts improve BOM 20-30% by Year 3

---

### Stream 3: Clinical & Institutional Licensing

**Product:** Therapist dashboards, cohort analytics, training
**Pricing:**
- Clinic license: $1,000/year per SLP
- Institutional license: $120/student/year (bulk districts)

**Assumptions:**
- Therapy channel launches Month 6 (Year 1)
- 10 clinics Year 1, 50 Year 2, 150 Year 3
- Institutional pilots (grant-funded) in Year 2, paid contracts Year 3

## Unit Economics: Consumer Subscription Deep Dive

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

**Blended CAC Calculation:**

| Channel | % of Customers | CAC per Customer | Weighted CAC |
|---------|----------------|------------------|--------------|
| Therapy Referral | 40% | $75 | $30 |
| Word-of-Mouth | 25% | $30 | $7.50 |
| Content Marketing (SEO, blog) | 20% | $120 | $24 |
| Paid Ads (Facebook, Google) | 10% | $300 | $30 |
| Conferences & Events | 5% | $200 | $10 |
| **Total Blended CAC** | **100%** | | **$101.50** |

**Conservative Blended CAC: $150-200** (includes operational overhead, failed experiments)

**Year 1 CAC:** $250 (high, early inefficiency)
**Year 2 CAC:** $180 (improving, therapy channel scaling)
**Year 3 CAC:** $140 (word-of-mouth + content maturity)

---

### Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)

**LTV Calculation Components:**

| Component | Value | Notes |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| **Average Subscription Price** | $40/month | Blended across Basic ($29), Premium ($49), Family ($79) |
| **Gross Margin (Subscription)** | 73% | Revenue $40 - COGS $11 = $29 gross profit |
| **Average Subscription Duration** | 15 months | Median user (Year 2-3, post-stabilization) |
| **Subscription LTV** | $435 | $40 × 15 months × 73% |
| **Hardware Gross Profit** | $100 | $300 revenue - $200 COGS |
| **Total LTV** | **$535** | Subscription + hardware |

**Conservative LTV: $535**
**Optimistic LTV (18mo retention): $656**

---

### LTV:CAC Ratio & Payback Period

**Year 1:**
- LTV: $435
- CAC: $250
- **LTV:CAC = 1.74:1** (unsustainable, expected for early stage)
- Payback: 9 months

**Year 2:**
- LTV: $535
- CAC: $180
- **LTV:CAC = 2.97:1** (healthy, approaching breakeven)
- Payback: 6 months

**Year 3:**
- LTV: $656 (improved retention)
- CAC: $140
- **LTV:CAC = 4.69:1** (excellent, sustainable growth)
- Payback: 5 months

**Benchmark:** SaaS best practice is 3:1 LTV:CAC ratio. Tizzy hits this by Year 2.

## Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)

### Hardware COGS (per unit)

| Component | Year 1 Cost | Year 3 Cost (Volume Discount) |
|-----------|-------------|-------------------------------|
| Bill of Materials (BOM) | $100 | $75 (-25%) |
| Manufacturing & Assembly | $60 | $50 |
| Packaging & Logistics | $15 | $12 |
| Quality Assurance | $10 | $8 |
| Warranty Reserve (5% failure) | $15 | $10 |
| **Total Hardware COGS** | **$200** | **$155** |

**Gross Margin:**
- Year 1: ($300 - $200) / $300 = **33%**
- Year 3: ($300 - $155) / $300 = **48%**

---

### Subscription COGS (per user per month)

| Component | Year 1 Cost | Year 3 Cost (Efficiency) |
|-----------|-------------|--------------------------|
| Cloud Compute (inference, storage) | $5 | $4 |
| Modulate API Fees | $3 | $2 (renegotiated) |
| Data Transfer & Bandwidth | $1 | $1 |
| Customer Support (amortized) | $2 | $1.50 |
| **Total Subscription COGS** | **$11** | **$8.50** |

**Gross Margin:**
- Year 1: ($40 - $11) / $40 = **73%**
- Year 3: ($40 - $8.50) / $40 = **79%**

---

### Blended Gross Margin

**Year 1:**
- Hardware: 33% × 24% of revenue = 7.9%
- Subscription: 73% × 15% of revenue = 10.9%
- Clinical: 90% × 13% of revenue = 11.7%
- Grants: 100% × 26% of revenue = 26%
- **Blended GM: ~56%**

**Year 2:**
- Hardware: 40% × 43% of revenue = 17.2%
- Subscription: 75% × 30% of revenue = 22.5%
- Clinical: 90% × 25% of revenue = 22.5%
- Institutional: 85% × 10% of revenue = 8.5%
- **Blended GM: ~64%**

**Year 3:**
- Hardware: 48% × 26% of revenue = 12.5%
- Subscription: 79% × 48% of revenue = 37.9%
- Clinical: 92% × 32% of revenue = 29.4%
- Institutional: 90% × 20% of revenue = 18%
- **Blended GM: ~68%**

**Target:** 65-70% gross margin by Year 3 (subscription-heavy mix, hardware efficiency)

## Sensitivity Analysis

### Scenario 1: Optimistic (Best Case)

**Assumptions:**
- Trial-to-paid conversion: 55% (vs. 40% base)
- Monthly churn: 4% (vs. 6% base)
- CAC: $120 (vs. $180 base, Year 2)

**Impact:**
- Year 2 Revenue: $1.5M (+44%)
- Year 3 Revenue: $5.2M (+49%)
- Break-even: Month 24 (end of Year 2)

---

### Scenario 2: Pessimistic (Worst Case)

**Assumptions:**
- Trial-to-paid conversion: 30% (vs. 40% base)
- Monthly churn: 10% (vs. 6% base)
- CAC: $250 (vs. $180 base, Year 2)

**Impact:**
- Year 2 Revenue: $650K (-38%)
- Year 3 Revenue: $2.1M (-40%)
- Break-even: Month 48+ (Year 4+)
- Requires additional funding (bridge round)

---

### Scenario 3: Delayed Institutional Adoption

**Assumptions:**
- Schools delay adoption (FERPA complexity, budget constraints)
- Institutional revenue in Year 3: $200K (vs. $700K base)

**Impact:**
- Year 3 Revenue: $3.0M (-14%)
- Year 3 EBITDA: -$1.43M (worse by $500K)
- Break-even delayed by 6 months

**Mitigation:** Focus on parent/therapy channels, don't rely on schools for near-term growth

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) to Track Weekly

### Revenue Metrics
- **MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue):** Primary growth indicator
- **ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue):** Year-over-year target (2x-3x growth)
- **New MRR:** From new customers + upsells
- **Churn MRR:** Lost from cancellations

### Unit Economics
- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):** By channel, blended
- **LTV (Lifetime Value):** Cohort-based analysis
- **LTV:CAC Ratio:** Target >3:1 by Year 2
- **Payback Period:** Target <9 months

### Product Metrics
- **Trial-to-Paid Conversion:** 40%+ target
- **Monthly Churn:** <7% (Year 1), <5% (Year 3)
- **Daily Active Users (DAU):** % wearing device 5+ days/week
- **Engagement:** Post-conversation review usage, app opens

### GTM Metrics
- **Therapy Partnerships:** 10 (Y1), 50 (Y2), 150 (Y3)
- **Referral Rate:** 25%+ of customers refer
- **NPS (Net Promoter Score):** 50+ target
- **Word-of-Mouth %:** % of new customers from organic referrals

## Conclusion: Conservative but Achievable

This financial model is built on:
- **Validated assumptions** from comparable products (health/wellness SaaS, assistive tech)
- **Conservative conversion and retention** (40% trial-to-paid, 6-7% monthly churn)
- **Realistic CAC** ($150-250 blended, improving over time)
- **Disciplined OpEx** (not overspending on unproven channels)

**The path to profitability is clear:**
1. Year 1: Prove value (pilot + therapy channel)
2. Year 2: Scale acquisition (parent adoption, word-of-mouth)
3. Year 3: Improve margins (hardware efficiency, subscription mix)
4. Year 4: Break-even and beyond

**This is not a moonshot. This is disciplined, mission-driven company building.**
