# Intro attribution term (template)

The plain-English term that goes with the intro ledger. Copy it per client, fill every
`{placeholder}` and `[NEED]`, and keep the copy next to that client's state file. The Sabo copy
is `~/Documents/Codex/2026-10-15/che/SABO-ATTRIBUTION-TERM.md`. A copy is a draft until both
sides agree to it in writing.

1. **What counts as an intro.** When we hand {client} a brand contact for a named creator,
   that's one intro. Each one is recorded with the brand, the person, their email, why we
   picked them, the creator it's for, and the date we delivered it.

2. **The delivered date.** The date the intro shows up in {client}'s table with a Delivered On
   stamp. Our ledger server's clock sets that date when the intro is recorded, and it can't be
   changed after.

3. **The window.** An intro counts for **[NEED] months** from its delivered date.

4. **What we get credit for.** Any paid deal between that brand and that creator that's signed
   inside the window, whoever ends up making the final contact. A renewal or extension of that
   same deal signed inside the window counts too. [NEED] whether a deal with the same brand
   for a different {client} creator also counts.

5. **What doesn't count.** A brand or person {client} was already talking to before our
   delivered date. {client} tells us within **[NEED] days** of getting the intro, with anything
   that shows the earlier contact (an email, a DM, a past contract), and we mark that intro as
   pre-existing with a correction record. If it isn't flagged in that time, it counts.

6. **The fee.** [NEED] percent of the deal value, paid when {client} or the creator gets paid by
   the brand.

7. **Telling us.** {client} lets us know when a deal from an intro is signed, with the brand,
   creator and deal value.

8. **Checking the record.** Either side can ask for the full list of intros at any time. We
   send it as a spreadsheet from the ledger (`/intros.csv?client={slug}`), and every row carries
   the same record number and code that's on the client's table, so both copies match line by
   line.

## Before sending a copy

- Check the client's own existing contacts against the recorded intros and mark any overlap
  pre-existing yourself, before they have to. On Sabo's first batch, Barebells was Scout Brown,
  a contact he already had.
- Confirm the client can see the table. If they can't yet, the clock started before they had
  the contacts.
