Amber
Three screens. People on the left, Today in the middle — a running feed of conversations, network updates and where you were, bound to the calendar — and You on the right.
Try this
  1. Hold the flame two seconds, then let go — it hears you, works out who it was about, and files it under Dana.
  2. Swipe a card in Today: left to dismiss it (your own memories ask first), right to keep it on someone’s card, tap to open the thread.
  3. In a thread, tap a numbered citation — each one is a contact card. Then ask a follow-up.
  4. People → Ask Amber: “Who do I know through Rosa?”, “Whose birthday is next?”
  5. You → tap People for the family tree, Locked for Face ID, or Share your card for the Wallet passes.

Built for a memory-impaired user: one gesture to capture, 44px+ targets, and no ambiguity about what was kept. The map is real OpenStreetMap geography, warm-graded to the paper. Monogram avatars stand in until you bring photographs — those would go through the system's plate treatment.
