2026-09-18. Found in `~/Downloads/Outbound change management review.md`, a
transcript of Sagar teaching Maggie Chen and Mike the framework he wants BMA to
run on. **Structure anything built for Sagar on these six, because he will
recognize his own scaffolding and because he said the goal is to build a campaign
for anyone inside 24 hours off six answers.**

**Internal, mostly fixed:** persona (whose voice, why anyone should care about
that person, how they establish authority), pricing, packaging.
**External, what changes per campaign:** accounts, signal/messaging, channel.

His load-bearing nuances, verbatim or close:

- **"You should not look at ICP builds and messaging as two distinct things.
  Instead, signals leading messaging."** The activation loop is signals, triggers
  and copy fused. This is the crux of the whole framework in his words.
- **Accounts are a preset TAM list that serves as a base for experimentation.**
  Many signals, roles and emails run off one account list. You do not rebuild it.
- **His feedback that killed Kevin's copy: "way too vague, both in terms of who
  we're targeting and in terms of who the message is coming from."**
- **"Don't get my emails fucking burned."** Deliverability is a named fear, not a
  technicality.
- **Filtering columns he named:** relationship to the sender, personal user
  behaviors, external data points. He then said **this is literally why Amber is
  being built**: a column saying you know these 26 people directly so text them,
  these are second degree, these are cold.
- **Pricing talk track:** Clay is a couple hundred a month, $800 is what they
  recommend, free POC or build out, retainers start around $4k a month.
- **Subject lines are stacked concrete nouns.** His demo: "private equity
  experienced data engineer reaching out to share some info about Structify."
- Workbook and folder names carry the packaging, as succinctly as possible.
