Caleb named the product Dough on 2026-10-28, after rejecting Sower, Doh and a list of clay-like words. The repo moved from calebnewtonusc/gtm-os to calebnewtonusc/dough (GitHub redirects the old link). He doesn't want "gtm" in any link.

Scope, in his words: "Dough isnt just for their gtm, its gonna have a ton of fire chewbs stuff and more". Outbound is the first piece, and Chewbacca's best tools come over as founders need them. Chewbacca stays the playground and isn't sold.

**The name isn't clear yet.** A USPTO search on 10-28, typed into tmsearch's own box, found two live DOUGH filings that overlap:

- Dough, Inc. (Delaware), serial 99863469, pending in classes 35 and 42, for business consulting for starting businesses
- tastylive, serial 88180023, registered in classes 9, 36 and 42, for financial software. It also holds dough.com

Free domains on 10-28: rundough.com, doughhq.com, getdough.ai, founderdough.com. None was bought, since a purchase needs Caleb's yes on the domain and price.

**How to apply:** don't put Dough on a site, deck or filing until a trademark lawyer has read those two, or Caleb picks a form that clears. When checking any name, run whois on the domains in parallel and type the word into tmsearch.uspto.gov's search box. Read the filter checkbox labels exactly, because matching on a parent element's text clicked every class filter and gave a false all-clear for Putty.
