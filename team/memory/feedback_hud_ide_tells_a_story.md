Caleb, 2026-10-11 8:29pm, after first seeing the agent tiles live: "It needs to be a digestible UX! Vscode and the claude app are ok at it, carlton's is pretty good! With the power of HUD we can make this literal storytelling level UX like the lemma site, each part of your screen being intentional, not just a sequence of lines".

**Why:** a transcript scroll is what every IDE already does; the glass can stage one beat at a time the way the uselemma.ai replica taught ([[feedback_website_four_layers]]).

**How to apply:** design an agent turn as beats (asked, reading, changing, proved, your call, done), give each a region of the glass, make the resting view one glance (what happened, what waits on him), keep the transcript one press away, subtract before adding. Builds on [[feedback_hud_agents_build_on_realm]]: our layer only, never Carlton's files.
