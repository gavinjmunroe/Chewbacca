Any product, vendor or platform name that goes on a site or into outreach ("works with Planning Center, Pushpay, ...") needs a source showing the audience actually uses it. Names said on a call are often placeholders: on the 2026-10-10 Firm Foundation call Caleb said "Church Connect or Churchly or whatever" and meant "we have to research this". The site's "synced both ways with" strip went out with an unsourced list and Caleb called it "a stupid assumption".

**Why:** a church staffer reads a wrong tool name as "they don't know my world", and it is the first thing they check.

**How to apply:** before a name ships, find usage evidence (surveys, vendor counts with dates, API docs) and keep it in the repo's research folder; until then, leave the name off. Treat any "X or Y or whatever" in a transcript as a research task, and log it as one. Related: [[feedback_client_review_is_the_gate]], [[project_firm_foundation_founding_2026_10_09]].
