On client work (Dough, any GTM client), don't stop at the ask. Keep going until the only
things left need a person's hands (a purchase, an account signup, an answer only the client
has), and put each of those on its own BLOCKED line.

Caleb, 2026-11-02, 10:16pm, during the AuraPath trial build: "Do everything we can tn, and
then go above and beyond and do more - dont stop until theres literally nothing that can be
done - this is the standard for work fix dough and chewbs especially for clients!"

The same night shows what "everything" covered once the domains were the only blocker: the
copy graded through gates 2 and 3, a second variant written, the grader taught the opt-out our
own copy uses, a paid buyer lookup added to Dough for ICP-fit rows with no named owner, a
setup checklist for the morning, and the client email sent. Each was something an agent could
do without Caleb. A stopping point that leaves one of those for him is too early.

How to apply: before a closing reply on client work, list what's left and sort it into
"needs his hands" and "I can do it". Do the second list, then report.
