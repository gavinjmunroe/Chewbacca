Amber is your network intelligence partner.

Ask Amber who you should talk to and Amber answers from your own private knowledge base about the people you know. It draws on Pinecone-backed memory, Solana-anchored identity, and a re-ranker called PeopleRank that understands context, not just keywords.

Bring your world in:
- Contacts sync keeps your graph accurate.
- Composio links Gmail, Google Calendar, and more so Amber knows who you met and when you are free.
- Exa finds people beyond your contacts when you need someone new.
- Apple Health and your calendar feed daily reflections across six dimensions of health.

Every answer cites the people and moments behind it. Every intro offers a warm path. Nothing ships to the cloud you did not authorize. You can audit, export, and delete your data at any time.

Amber is for founders, operators, students, and caregivers who treat relationships as the work. Not a rolodex. Not a CRM. A second brain for the people you love.
