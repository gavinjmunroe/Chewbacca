# ACAD 185 course map

ACAD 185, Analytics and Marketing for Challenge-Based Innovation, Prof. Chris
Swain, USC Iovine and Young Academy, Fall 2026. Each lecture, what it
teaches, and the Chewbacca skill that carries it. The decks, assignment
briefs and templates are paid course material and live only in the private
brain at `raw/acad185/` (decks, briefs, templates, and a notes file per
lecture). Never copy them into the kit.

- W1S1 Course Intro: why validate with data before building. venture-validation.
- W1S2 Semester Project Ideation: inflection theory and Why Now, nested S-curves, exponential technologies. venture-validation step 1.
- W2S3 Network Effects and Power Laws: Y = M x X^B, superlinear growth, defensibility. venture-validation step 1.
- W2S4 What Investors Look For: types of capital, the VC model (2 and 20, 3x a fund, 100x per deal, 85% fail), six things VCs screen for, the self validation dashboard. venture-validation step 1.
- W3S5 The Mind of the VC: staged investing, cap tables, valuation by ARR multiple, preferred stock, pro rata, liquidation preferences. startup-finance.
- W3S6 The Mom Test: signs before calls, the seven question rules, recorded calls, false positives. venture-validation step 3.
- W4S7 Diffusion of Innovation: the five adoption criteria, adopter stages, beachhead and superfans. venture-validation step 2.
- W4S8 Validation Metrics and B2C Dashboard: traffic, conversion, validation; CTR, L-CR, R-CR, CPL, CPR, eROAS, CAC; the levers per stage. venture-validation step 4 and bin/funnel.py.
- W5S9 Consumer Based Brand Equity and W5S10 CBBE Lab: Keller's pyramid from salience to resonance, points of parity and difference. launch-funnel step 2.
- W6S11 Team Project Presentations: the 4 minute pitch structure. venture-validation (the Kyber A9 deck is the worked example).
- W6S12 Social Artifact Creation 1: what the algorithm rewards, hooks, the niche, brand vs community vs founder accounts. community-content.
- W7S13 Social Artifact Creation 2: the 80/20 rule, the CMS columns, the content bank, synthesize and simulate, posting. community-content.
- W8S15 Messaging and Direction: personas, the messaging framework, the MDS. launch-funnel steps 1 to 3.
- W8S16 and W9S18 Visual Storytelling 1 and 2: contrast and affinity across space, line, shape, tone, color, movement and rhythm; color meanings; building the funnel. launch-funnel steps 4 to 7.
- A16 reading, Seth Godin "The Tribes We Lead": lead people who already want something. community-content.

Worked examples on Caleb's machine: the A10 dashboard
