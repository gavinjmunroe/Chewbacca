Firm Foundation's site (firmfoundation.tech, site/ in gavinjmunroe/firm-foundation) is
read by church staff: the exec pastor, the ops director, the lead pastor's assistant who
clicked through from our email and might forward it to the lead pastor. Caleb, 2026-10-18:
"this is for church staff not for instagram it should look cool but in a way that also
still is great to church staff."

**Why:** the motion and design passes keep drifting toward what impresses designers and
reel viewers. A staffer deciding whether to trust two college founders with their church's
data needs to find service-style clarity fast: what we do, that a person approves every
word, and how to start.

**How to apply:** before shipping any site, video or founder copy for FF, read it as a
50 year old office admin on a church laptop. Motion stays calm and purposeful, copy stays
plain and warm, nothing flashy that slows them reaching the ask. Founder statements stay
plain like Gavin's (about.founders in site/lib/content.ts). Related:
[[project_firm_foundation_site]], [[project_firm_foundation_site_motion_split]].
