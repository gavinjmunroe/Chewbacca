Applied ML Engineer

Full timeNew York or remote, USResearch

You will build the graders that decide whether a run went wrong, and you will be the person who knows how often they are right.

What you would do

  • Design checks that link each claim in an answer to the step that supports it.
  • Build labelled sets from real customer runs and keep them honest as products change.
  • Measure precision and recall per failure type, and publish the numbers to customers.
  • Decide when a plain rule beats a model, and say so in writing.

What we look for

  • You have trained or evaluated models on messy production data, not only benchmarks.
  • Python, a working knowledge of how LLM agents call tools, and an instinct for where evals lie.
  • You write up a negative result as carefully as a positive one.

How we hire

A thirty minute call, a paid half-day project grading a sample of anonymised runs, and a conversation with the whole team about what you found.
