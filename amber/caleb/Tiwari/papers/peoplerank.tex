\begin{abstract}
We formalize \textsc{tiwari-peoplerank} as a six-channel
personalized random walk on the Amber relationship graph, one channel
per dimension of health (spiritual, emotional, physical, intellectual,
social, financial). The six per-dimension stationary distributions
$\pi^{(d)}$ are fused on a joint manifold to produce a single ranking
that respects both semantic relevance to a query and the dimensional
profile of the querying user. We prove convergence, give a contraction
bound, and describe how the algorithm braids together three evidence
streams: \textsc{togari} (web-scale, scraped), \textsc{newton}
(user-specific signal heuristics), and \textsc{amber-id} (the user's
private Pinecone knowledge graph).
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}

Let $G = (V, E, W)$ be a directed, weighted graph where
$V$ is the union of every person referenced in the user's amber-id
knowledge base, every contact in the user's Togari-ingested contact
list, and the user themselves (the \emph{ego}).
Each edge $(u,v)\in E$ carries a stochastic-row weight
$w_{uv}\ge 0$ with $\sum_v w_{uv} = 1$.

We define six dimensions
$\mathcal{D} = \{\text{spi},\text{emo},\text{phy},\text{int},\text{soc},\text{fin}\}$
and six \emph{edge-weight sheets} $W^{(d)}\in\mathbb{R}^{|V|\times|V|}$
with $W^{(d)}_{uv}$ equal to the relative evidence that the
relationship $(u\to v)$ contributes to dimension $d$. For a
conversation about a medical scare, $W^{(\text{phy})}$ and
$W^{(\text{emo})}$ spike; for a job referral, $W^{(\text{fin})}$
dominates.

\section{Personalized Teleport Vector}

Given a natural-language query $q$ issued by ego $e$, we classify $q$
into a dimension-weight simplex
\[
\bm{\alpha}(q) \in \Delta^5, \qquad \sum_{d\in\mathcal{D}} \alpha_d(q) = 1
\]
via an embedding classifier (see \texttt{amber-id/src/lib/dimension-classifier.js}).

We assemble a per-dimension personalization vector
$\bm{v}^{(d)}\in\mathbb{R}^{|V|}$ by seeding the ego and boosting any
vertex $p\in V$ that amber-id's Pinecone namespace returns for the
query, weighted by the Pinecone cosine similarity $s_p$ and by the
metadata-level trust score $\tau_p\in[0,1]$ that the ingestion pipe
assigns (agent $=0.65$, health $=0.95$, check-in $=1.00$,
relationship $=0.50$).

\begin{equation}
v^{(d)}_p \propto
\mathbb{1}[p=e] + \lambda \cdot s_p \cdot \tau_p \cdot W^{(d)}_{ep},
\qquad
\sum_p v^{(d)}_p = 1.
\end{equation}

\section{Six Random Walks, One per Dimension}

Each dimension gets its own personalized PageRank stationary
distribution $\pi^{(d)}$, defined by the fixed-point equation
\begin{equation}
\pi^{(d)} = (1 - \beta)\, \bm{v}^{(d)}
          + \beta \, (P^{(d)})^\top \pi^{(d)},
\qquad \beta \in (0,1),
\end{equation}
where $P^{(d)}$ is the row-stochastic transition matrix derived from
$W^{(d)}$.

\begin{proposition}[Existence, uniqueness, contraction]
For every $\beta \in (0,1)$ and every personalization
$\bm{v}^{(d)}\in\Delta^{|V|-1}$, the map
$T^{(d)}(\pi)\triangleq (1-\beta)\bm{v}^{(d)} + \beta(P^{(d)})^\top\pi$
is a $\beta$-contraction on $(\Delta^{|V|-1},\|\cdot\|_1)$. Therefore
$\pi^{(d)}$ exists and is unique, and power-iteration converges
geometrically with rate $\beta$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For $\pi, \pi' \in \Delta^{|V|-1}$,
$\|T^{(d)}(\pi) - T^{(d)}(\pi')\|_1
 = \beta\, \| (P^{(d)})^\top (\pi - \pi') \|_1
 \le \beta\, \|\pi - \pi'\|_1$,
since a row-stochastic matrix is a non-expansion under $\ell_1$.
\end{proof}

\section{Joint-Manifold Fusion}

We fuse the six distributions using the query-conditioned simplex
$\bm{\alpha}(q)$ together with a \emph{dimension-diversity} term that
penalizes rankings that collapse onto one channel:

\begin{equation}
\label{eq:fusion}
\pi^\star(p \mid q)
 \;\propto\;
 \underbrace{\prod_{d\in\mathcal{D}} \bigl(\pi^{(d)}_p\bigr)^{\alpha_d(q)}}_{\text{weighted geometric mean}}
 \cdot
 \underbrace{\exp\!\Bigl(\gamma \cdot H\bigl(\bm{\pi}_p\bigr)\Bigr)}_{\text{diversity reward}},
\end{equation}
where $\bm{\pi}_p \in \Delta^5$ is the $\ell_1$-normalized row of
per-dimension scores for vertex $p$, $H$ is Shannon entropy, and
$\gamma \ge 0$ is a diversity temperature.

The geometric mean is the natural fusion on the probability simplex
(it is the minimizer of the Aitchison distance to the six inputs).
The entropy bonus embodies the product philosophy: a person who shows
up across multiple dimensions of a user's life is more valuable to
rank high than one who dominates a single channel.

\section{Braiding in Togari and Newton}

Equation~\ref{eq:fusion} scores vertices on the amber-id manifold
alone. The production ranking blends in two external evidence streams:

\begin{equation}
\label{eq:braid}
\text{score}(p, q)
 \;=\;
 \omega_T \cdot \tilde s^T_p
 + \omega_N \cdot \tilde s^N_p
 + \omega_A \cdot \tilde\pi^\star_p,
\end{equation}
where the three inputs are min-max normalized to $[0,1]$ on the
candidate set, and $(\omega_T, \omega_N, \omega_A)$ are adapter
weights. The production default is
$(\omega_T, \omega_N, \omega_A) = (0.55, 0.20, 0.25)$,
reflecting that Togari's list-building corpus is the densest prior,
Newton contributes ego-specific heuristics, and amber-id's private
manifold breaks ties with the user's actual life.

\section{Identifiability and Privacy}

The random walk respects two hard constraints:

\begin{enumerate}
\item A vertex with metadata \texttt{visibility = "private"} may
      contribute to $\pi^{(d)}$ only when the query originates from
      the owning ego. Cross-ego reads are masked before the walk.
\item Trust-weighted teleport ensures that scraped (Togari, trust
      $\approx 0.65$) evidence never outranks first-person check-ins
      (trust $= 1.00$) for the same person at the same query.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Complexity}

With $|V|=n$, $|E|=m$, and $k$ power-iteration steps to reach
$\epsilon$ accuracy, the per-query cost is $O(k \cdot |\mathcal{D}| \cdot m)$.
In practice $k \le \lceil \log(1/\epsilon)/\log(1/\beta) \rceil$ and
the six dimensions run in parallel, giving an effective
$O(k\cdot m)$ wall-clock with a six-way parallel harness.

\section{Why This Is the Right Form}

A flat PageRank on one graph cannot distinguish \emph{why} person $p$
matters to the user. Six channels let Amber answer
``who should I talk to for my career?'' and
``who should I call because I'm sad?'' with different people, using
the same graph. The dimension-diversity term ensures Amber never
reduces a human to a single utility.
