Firm Foundation outbound and public copy pair every stat or site finding with what it costs that reader, in terms they already feel. A bare number or a bare "your address is in the footer" invites "isn't that ok?"

**Why:** Caleb, 2026-11-05, on the church website study: "Some of those things you're not making clear that they're a problme, someoene could be like its in the footer isnt that ok?", then "this should inform outbound moving forward - we can't say 'XYZ stat' if they don't feel how that's a problem".

**How to apply:** Before a line goes in an email or on a page, read it as the pastor and ask "so what?" If the line doesn't answer that, add the cost to a guest or to the staff. Example from /research/church-websites: the address is about 5 phone screens down, and a family in the car has to scroll for it.
