FAQ
Does the free trial require a credit card?
No card required for the 14-day trial on Solo or Pro. Team trials require a card on file, charged only at conversion. The Free plan has no trial period and no card, ever.
What happens when I exceed my monthly request limit?
Requests above the plan ceiling are queued and billed at $2.00 per 1,000 at the next cycle. You can set a hard cap in account settings to reject overages with a 429 instead. No automatic plan upgrades.
Can I switch plans mid-cycle?
Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated to the day. Downgrades apply at next renewal. Data and keys are preserved across changes within a tier. Downgrading below a feature tier disables but does not delete those resources for 30 days.
How does annual billing work?
Annual plans are charged once upfront at a 20% discount against the monthly rate. Prorated refunds for unused months are available on request within 60 days of payment. Add-ons remain on monthly billing regardless of base plan cadence.
Is there a discount for open-source projects or students?
Solo is free for verified maintainers of open-source projects with 250 or more GitHub stars. Students with a .edu address receive Solo at 50% off. Both require annual re-verification. Apply in account settings under Billing.
Where is data stored, and what law governs it?
Default region is us-east-1. EU customers may select eu-west-1 at signup; region cannot be changed after account creation without migration support. Data processing is governed by the DPA linked in the footer. SOC 2 Type II report available under NDA on request.
Can I export all my data before canceling?
Full account exports in JSON and CSV are available in settings on all plans. Free plan exports are limited to 90 days of history. Exports are delivered by email within 24 hours. After cancellation, data is retained for 30 days before deletion.
What counts as a "request" against the monthly limit?
Every authenticated API call to any endpoint counts as one request, regardless of payload size or response size. Webhook deliveries do not count. Health checks to /ping do not count. Failed requests due to client error (4xx) do count.
