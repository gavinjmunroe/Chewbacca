## Key Strategic Insights

### The Moat is the Dataset

- Tizzy's defensible advantage: 10,000+ hours of labeled autism conversational data
- No competitor has this (18-24 month head start to replicate)
- Clinical partnerships validate outcomes, build trust, drive distribution
- Privacy-first architecture differentiates from "creepy tech" competitors

### Phased GTM Minimizes Risk

**Wave 1 (Months 0-6):** College students (simple consent, high pain point)
**Wave 2 (Months 6-12):** Therapy channel (clinical credibility, warm leads)
**Wave 3 (Months 12-24):** Parent-driven adoption (word-of-mouth, proven outcomes)
**Wave 4 (Months 24+):** Schools/institutions (heavy compliance, long cycles)

### Unit Economics are Sustainable

- LTV:CAC ratio improves from 1.7:1 (Y1) → 3.0:1 (Y2) → 4.7:1 (Y3)
- Payback period: 9 months (Y1) → 5 months (Y3)
- Gross margins: 56% (Y1) → 68% (Y3) as subscription mix increases
- Break-even by Month 30-36 (early Year 4)

### Privacy is the Product, Not a Policy

- Default: on-device processing, no raw audio retention
- Transparent data governance dashboard (users see exactly what's captured)
- Bystander protection (focus on wearer behavior)
- Hard mute and consent receipts
- Differentiates from Friend pendant backlash
