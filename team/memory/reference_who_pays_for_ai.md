Checked 2026-10-13 for the consumer lane (report: ~/.chewbacca/overnight/d1013/REPORT-normal-person.md on Caleb's Mac).

- Menlo Ventures' 2026 consumer AI report (Sept 15, 5,067 US adults): 64% of US adults use AI and 55% of those pay for at least one AI product, about 90 million Americans, roughly 1 in 3 adults. The "2 to 3% pay" figure Caleb quoted was Menlo's 2025 estimate across the world's 1.8 billion users.
- Pew Research (Feb 2026, 5,119 adults): half of US adults never use a chatbot. Of them, 83% aren't interested, 79% worry about privacy, 76% don't trust the answers, 55% don't know how to use it. Cost barely shows up.

**How to apply:** pitch non-users a job they already do every day, done where they already are (texts, calendar, email), with the privacy line said plainly, never "AI". The Claude-subscription positioning in [[project_chewbacca_positioning]] only reaches people who already pay. Re-check the numbers before they go in anything public.
