Source: x.com/michlieben/status/2107499997906514147 (X article, 2026-10-06). Caleb sent it 2026-10-07. Author runs ColdIQ and the piece ends in a ColdIQ plug; stats are vendor reports.

What applies to a private note: lists of 500 to 1,000 with one play each (20-30% replies vs 2-3% on 100k blasts), 30 sends per inbox per day max ramped 10/20/30, bounces under 3%, email 1 plain text with no links and tracking off (Hunter: +68% replies), offer CTA over meeting ask (Gong: +28% vs -44%), 3-step sequence with empty subjects on 2 and 3. Tiering by closed-won maps to investors who already wrote checks in the sector.
