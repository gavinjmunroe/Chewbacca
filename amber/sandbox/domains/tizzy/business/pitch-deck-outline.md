### SLIDE 5: HOW IT WORKS (Product Demo Flow)

**Visual:**
- Four-step user journey with icons/illustrations:

1. **Wear the Device**
   - Necklace form factor (discreet, comfortable)
   - 24-hour battery, always-on capture with privacy controls

2. **Conversations Happen**
   - Device captures audio, processes locally into acoustic features
   - Real-time analysis: pace, tone, interruptions, topic shifts

3. **In-the-Moment Feedback**
   - Haptic patterns:
     - Single pulse → pacing adjustment
     - Double pulse → ask a return question
     - Long pulse → escalation risk, consider exiting

4. **Post-Conversation Review**
   - App shows timeline of moments that matter
   - "What went well" + "What to improve"
   - One micro-goal for tomorrow

**Talking Points:**
- **Privacy-first:** Raw audio processed on-device, not stored by default.
- **Coaching, not judgment:** Focus on growth, not fixing who you are.
- **Personalized:** Learns your baseline, adapts to your goals.

**Leave-Behind Message:** Simple, effective, respectful.

### SLIDE 6: THE SCIENCE (Why This Works Now)

**Visual:**
- Split-screen comparison:

**Left Side: Transcript-Only AI**
- Text: "That's great."
- Interpretation: Positive ✓
- Reality: Sarcasm (MISSED)

**Right Side: Voice-First AI (Modulate ELM)**
- Text: "That's great."
- Audio signal: Flat affect, monotone, no pitch variation
- Interpretation: Likely sarcasm or disinterest ✓
- Reality: Correct

**Talking Points:**
- **Why now:** Voice AI is moving beyond "speech-to-text + LLM."
- Transcript-only systems lose **prosody, timing, tone, intensity**—the "how it was said" layer.
- Tizzy uses **Modulate's Ensemble Listening Model (ELM)**: voice-first understanding that combines words + acoustic signals.
- **10x more cost-effective** than OpenAI/GPT-4 for voice understanding.
- **Autism-specific coaching models** trained on real-world data (our moat).

**Leave-Behind Message:** The technology is finally ready. Tizzy is building the autism-first application layer.

## PITCH DELIVERY NOTES

### Opening (30 seconds)
- Start with the problem, not the product.
- "Imagine you're autistic. You know conversations break down, but you don't know why."

### Middle (10-12 minutes)
- Keep it conversational, not scripted.
- Show product demo video (if available).
- Reference real user testimonials.

### Closing (2 minutes)
- Reinforce the moat (dataset + clinical partnerships + trust).
- Be clear about the ask ($1.5-2M seed).
- End with urgency: "We're closing the round in [X] weeks."

### Q&A (5-10 minutes)
- Anticipate common questions:
  - "What if parents don't want their kids recorded?"
  - "How do you compete with Friend pendant or Limitless?"
  - "What's your take rate on subscriptions?"
  - "Why can't OpenAI just do this?"
- Have crisp, evidence-based answers.
