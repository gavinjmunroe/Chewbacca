Hypothesis, not a rule. Caleb, 2026-11-05: "Wait what abt the church's mission/beliefs/etc. thats rlly important right for the first page??? And info abt the lead pastor and pastor's wife". His models are epicentre.org, upperroom.co, cachurch.com and generationsla.org/about. Then, when an agent shipped it as a fix the same turn: "Dont take what I say and give you as bible!"

**Why:** The evidence is mixed. In Grey Matter's 2012 survey 22% of visitors checked beliefs, behind service times at 43% and directions at 28%. His favorite sites don't all pass the study's 10 checks, from a quick skim that no reviewer coded. A spouse check can't be universal, since many churches, Catholic parishes among them, won't have one.

**How to apply:** Say your own read of his design takes before building them, with the strongest reason against and what would settle it. Settle this one with people: show about 20 strangers an identity-led first screen and a logistics one, and ask which church they'd visit and why. A rough script count of home page links on 1,023 sites found a beliefs link on 48% and a staff or pastor link on 65%; nobody reviewed it, so don't publish it.
