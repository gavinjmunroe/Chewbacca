Features

Everything your social presence needs

One tab handles what used to take six different tools, three browser tabs, and a spreadsheet.

🗓️

Visual Content Calendar

Drag and drop your posts across any time slot. See your entire month at a glance, spot gaps before they cost you reach.

⚡

Best Time to Post

Postly analyzes your audience data and surfaces the windows where your posts will actually be seen, per platform, per account.

🤖

AI Caption Generator

Describe the post. Get five caption options with hashtag suggestions, tuned to your brand voice and platform norms.

📊

Cross-Platform Analytics

Reach, engagement, follower growth, and link clicks in one view. No more hopping between native dashboards that lie about each other.

👥

Team Collaboration

Assign drafts, leave comments, and run an approval workflow before anything goes live. Built for agencies and in-house teams both.

🔄

Bulk Scheduling

Upload a CSV or connect a Notion database and watch a month of content drop into your queue in under two minutes.

How it works

Live in ten minutes

Connect your accounts, drop in your content, and walk away. Postly handles the timing.

1

Connect your channels

OAuth in one click for Instagram, X, LinkedIn, TikTok, Facebook, Pinterest, and YouTube. No API keys, no developer portal.

2

Create or import your posts

Write in the editor, paste from a doc, or upload a CSV. Media handles up to 4K video, every aspect ratio, batch format conversion included.

3

Schedule and review

Set it and forget it, or review everything in the queue before it goes live. Every post shows a platform-accurate preview before it ships.
