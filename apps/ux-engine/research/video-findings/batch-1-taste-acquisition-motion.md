**Two of the three most on-topic videos in the batch are empty files.** `RbDnsAsp0WE` (Jenny Wen, Anthropic design lead, "Craft is Counter-Intuitive") and `bWffMLkBQLU` (Thiago Costa, "Cultivating taste as a designer") are both 0 bytes at `corpus/video/transcripts/`. **The single most lintable rule in the batch is from 2017 and is not about design at all, it is an algorithm.** Jesse Showalter's alphabet audit at [5:58] is a full procedure: enumerate every visual variant on a page, letter each distinct one, consolidate, target at most six classes. **Ingbretson's mechanism is ranking, not looking.** I expected "look at a lot of art." What he actually describes is maintaining a persistent top-ten shortlist of physical objects, adding candidates, and forcing eviction, [14:03] "i think i've outdone those." That is pairwise preference learning with a bounded buffer, arrived at in a shoebox. He also insists at [8:34] on judging "even without knowledge," which inverts the usual advice to study before opining.

**Studying a master's worst paintings raises the estimate of the master.** q7AzRHoi4wo [10:03]: "when you juxtapose a great painter with his faults it's amazing how great they become." I would have predicted the opposite.
