The Firm Foundation home hero (site/lib/motion/heroScene.ts) was ported from the Lemma replica: tabbed plates with sheet numbers ("FF. 1.1" for "LEM. 1.1"), a brick wall whose lit stones widen sideways, narrow to squares and flip into diamonds on an iso lattice, and a timed wave of rising cubes. On 2026-10-31 Caleb said it looked "like a complete rip off of lemma", and that he has friends who know Lemma.

Turning the bricks into pills was rejected the same hour: "making it ovals isnt intentional especially since ur still copying the animation of them expanding sideways and then turning into squares on a grid". And: "the lemma one fit the lemma story well, we need one that fits the firm foundation one".

**Why:** people in his circle know Lemma's site, so a copied choreography reads as copied whatever the shapes are.

**How to apply:** on any FF scene, start from FF's story (Matthew 7:24-27, the house on the sand vs. the rock, is the name itself; Caleb floated sand particles washing away on 10/31), write the beats, then pick motion. Never keep a reference site's sequence of moves and swap its shapes. Arched window plates named on the sill and the staked-and-strung foundation layout were kept as ours (branch feat/hero-own-look).
