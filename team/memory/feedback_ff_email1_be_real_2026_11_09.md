Firm Foundation's cold email 1 to a church follows the version Gavin rewrote line by line on 2026-11-09 and sent to Northview Baptist. He said the old copy needed to be more real, and that both its hook and its second paragraph were bad.

What changed, each from his own words that afternoon:

- The opener says what happened when we did what a guest does, in first person and plain words ("I pulled up Northview's site on my phone to find Sunday service times. The only one I saw was 12:45, and that's the Spanish service."). No audit words like homepage, footer screens or "a guest skimming".
- No insider names the reader has to decode. He cut REDES for "the Spanish service": "nobody knows what redes is, dumb it down".
- Para 2 says we build church websites and the systems behind them, so it touches more than web design: "we need to touch more on our other services, not just web design". His ask was "agentic backend to help connect the church with their community like never before... in an easy and professional way". It shipped as "the systems behind them that keep a church connected to every new face in its community". Never "agentic" or "state of the art" in church copy. His exact wording of those scored worst on best-pass.
- Proof is "We've done work for Durango Vineyard and staff at UPPERROOM Dallas." He removed Chase by name ("dont mention chase, only staff at upperroom dallas"). The UPPERROOM work is Chase's personal site, so "work for UPPERROOM" alone overclaims. Mixbook and White's Chapel are out.
- He signs "Gavin Munroe" with the tagline "Firm Foundation, bringing churches closer to their communities" under it (plan sender.tagline).
- The ask is "I wrote down a few other spots on {{short}}'s where a first-time guest could slip through. Want me to send them over?"

Dough's 125-word cap counts the footer, and the tagline plus address and stop line take about 30 of those words. So the opener plus the fixed body has to land near 95 words, and the opener gets about 35. His okayed draft came to 139 and the Christmas line got cut to fit.

best-pass wobbles 0.02 to 0.03 run to run, and every version of this email sat at 0.50 to 0.55 on "fits this reader now". So don't tune copy to that read once it's at the line. His read decides.
