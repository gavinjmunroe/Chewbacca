On 2026-10-31 the first build of the FF home redesign from Colin's call checked boxes literally:
- a 1-2-3 word section that duplicated Website, Follow-up and Back end,
- a rock that faded in and covered 1% of the sand,
- sine streams instead of a wave,
- no church point-of-view pan,
- floating church props explained by labels.

Caleb: "you literally made an ai slop 1 2 3 animation", "you removed all the meat", "put yourself in my pov... be systematic". The rebuild that held up did four things:
- wrote site/HOME-SPEC.md from every line of the call;
- watched the reference video before building (jayd3nfx's triple wheel);
- used the kit's film vocabulary (library/crafts/motion-film.md and the FilmCraft transition math);
- kept the existing meat, like the ChurchApp demo, and restyled around it.

**Why:** A call's offhand phrases ("pan to the side of the church", "the wave brushes the sand away", "FF comes in, bam") describe camera moves and morphs. Read as a list, they come out as fades and labels.

**How to apply:** Before building from a call:
1. Write a SPEC with every line in it.
2. Watch every reference link sent in the thread after the call, not just the transcript.
3. Name the camera move, the transition and the object each beat morphs from.
4. Check nothing that already worked got removed.
