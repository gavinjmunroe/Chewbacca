Caleb, 2026-10-07, on the Amber fridge draft: "Make sure pencil and pen look like pencil and pen, and make sure based on the surface they are on they actually look like that surface. Whenever you make smth, ask the questions that will lead you to concluding things like this." The night before: "The handwriting is too perfect bruh" and "Never make perfect handwriting."

**Why:** a handwriting font set straight onto a page, with the same ink on every surface, read as AI to him at a glance. The fix came from asking what made each mark and what it sits on, which nobody had asked.

**How to apply:** the question list lives in Chewbacca `skills/interface/SKILL.md` under "Ask what it is made of before drawing it". Answer those before drawing any object. Related: [[reference_nalana_is_blender]], [[feedback_tasteful_editorial_is_the_new_slop]].
