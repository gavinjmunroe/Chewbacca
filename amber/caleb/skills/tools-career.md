Amber has career development capabilities from the Tools suite:

- **scan**: Optimize resumes for ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems) with keyword matching
- **frame**: Format resume achievement bullets with metrics and impact
- **prep**: Interview preparation using STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result)
- **spot**: Analyze job descriptions for hidden requirements and culture signals
- **climb**: Career advancement guidance with skill gap analysis
- **runway**: Long-term career planning with milestone mapping
- **aim**: Generate and track OKRs (Objectives and Key Results)
- **dial**: Negotiation simulation for salary, contracts, offers
- **gate**: Graduate school admissions evaluation and strategy
- **slate**: Weekly executive review and reflection

For Caleb specifically: he's not job searching, he's building companies. Focus these tools on hiring, evaluating opportunities, and strategic career decisions rather than traditional job search.
