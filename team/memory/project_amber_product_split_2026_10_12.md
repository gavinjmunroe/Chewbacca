Amber weekly standup, 2026-10-12 (Caleb, Sagar, Karthik, Shirley). Builds on
[[project_amber_roles_2026_10_11]] and [[project_kyber_amber_keyboard]].

Product split, as Karthik stated it and the team agreed:

- The iOS app is the main hub, with the deepest record of a person's network.
- The keyboard and Amber Web each get a narrow job. Sagar wants the keyboard
  simple enough for Shirley to test with hundreds of consumers, ideas being one
  sharp use case like payment splitting. Caleb argued for a keyboard that does
  everything with a cleaner UX and was outvoted: "a few things really well".
- No iMessage agent. Karthik called it negative ROI ("Jesus Christ, this
  again"), Caleb agreed it splits focus. Agents in Slack and WhatsApp prove
  value and funnel people toward the app.

Karthik writes three PRDs (keyboard, iOS, Amber Web) in Sagar's note format:
purpose, scope, workflow, requirements, data, quality, operating. The team
thumbs them up or down in writing, then Caleb and Karthik build.

Sagar's standing question, which he said he'll ask every day: how does one
Amber flow (1) get dogfed so the team never loses anyone who enters its
system, (2) handle enterprise reply management, with the Zeutara replies
landing in Slack as the example, and (3) give Shirley a consumer entry point
for content. Caleb and Karthik owe a written answer on how custom instances
work across Slack, WhatsApp and every iMessage group-chat permutation.

Shirley owns top of funnel: a weekly personal-brand content schedule in
writing, and an Amber brand strategy deck with channels, KPIs, sample assets
and monthly burn (Sagar's frame: how she'd spend $20K a month).

**How to apply:** check the PRDs before building keyboard or web features;
anything that widens the keyboard's scope contradicts this call.
