A site never shows a wall of text: a block holds 45 words at most and a section
120 words of body copy, long reading goes behind a link or into an `<article>`
page, and `site-gate check` fails both limits.

**Why:** 2026-10-10, Erik Fish's draft home page carried his whole about page and
six article excerpts. Caleb: "Chewbacca should never make fat walls of text!
Ever! terrible ux. IF ppl wanted to read a book they would open a book, they're
on a website for a reason!!!!!!!!!" and "Fix chewbs bro".

**How to apply:** tell each section as beats (one line per scroll step),
figures or real objects. Cut the owner's copy to the strongest sentence and link
the rest. Related: [[feedback_website_four_layers]], [[feedback_site_research_pass]].
