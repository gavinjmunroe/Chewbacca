**Amber is "a decentralized, secure instance of your most important assets: your health and your people."** Sagar Tiwari said this publicly in May 2026, framing it against the claim that as AI improves the phone becomes your digital passport. Amber is what the passport carries. The name and framing came out of Perplexity's Billion Dollar Business competition, where Sagar tagged Caleb.

**The rule that follows: Amber is not a CRM, and he says so explicitly.** "Every CRM ever built is the corporate version of that same problem, badly solved. We measure pipeline stages. Not whether the person on the other end feels remembered." His own framing of the product is "a common save file for the people who make you happier and healthier." The bottleneck he is fixing is memory, not contact storage.

**Why:** the Medha/Togari split, the phones, and the tenant IDs are learning-phase implementation. They describe how Amber is built, not what it is. Context that only records the architecture leaves the actual product undefined, which is how a relationship-memory system drifts back into being a CRM.

**How to apply:** before any Amber, Medha, Togari, or BMA product decision, read `~/second-brain/projects/amber/what-amber-is.md` and `sagar-doctrine.md`. If a proposed feature makes Amber more CRM-shaped, that is the wrong direction. The full folder holds BMA's numbers and org design, Sagar's ten operating beliefs, his 2026 timeline (business school starts ~Sept 2026), and the verbatim LinkedIn corpus all of it was sourced from.
