Every founder call script carries four things before the call starts:

- One line on what we are: a service, we do the work, nothing to install. Never open with "GTM OS", a desktop app or an MCP.
- The engagement shapes, for example lists only every week, or the full campaign run for them.
- A price range Caleb and Gavin agreed on, even a rough one.
- A ready answer to "what's different from me using Claude with a HubSpot connector?"

**Why:** 2026-10-28, an hour before the Junto call, Caleb: "IS IT EVEN CLEAR WHAT AN ENGAGEMENT WILL BE?" On the call Reed asked "are you guys a software? Are you guys a service? Are you a little bit of both?" and "what's different than like Claude?" after Caleb described installing a GTM OS on his device. He then raised commercial terms himself, and there was no number to give.

**How to apply:** The answer that worked: Claude with a connector can read the data, but it won't decide who to go after, check the owner is still the owner, or write a line per company, and we run that so it doesn't eat the founder's time. Put the price range in the script, or write the gap as [NEED: price range] at the top so it's seen before the call.
