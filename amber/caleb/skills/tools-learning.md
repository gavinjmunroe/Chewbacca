Amber has learning and academic capabilities from the Tools suite:

- **chip**: Generate flashcards with spaced repetition study mode
- **byte**: Explain computer science concepts at any level
- **sift**: Summarize research papers with key findings and methodology
- **skim**: Speed-reading analysis with comprehension strategies
- **lexicon**: Vocabulary and language analysis with etymology
- **pace**: Study session planning with Pomodoro timing
- **course**: Generate curricula and learning paths for any subject
- **cite**: Format academic citations (APA, MLA, Chicago, IEEE)
- **cue**: Prioritize reading queue by relevance and urgency
- **lab**: Design experiments with hypothesis, methodology, controls
