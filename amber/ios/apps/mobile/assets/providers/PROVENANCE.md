# Provider marks

Verified September 24, 2026. These marks identify integrations; they do not imply a partnership or that an account is connected.

- **Google**: `google-official.png` is the unmodified standalone gradient G published by Google at https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/g-logo.png, linked from [Google Identity branding guidelines](https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines). It replaces the monochrome FontAwesome icon. Keep its original colors/aspect ratio on a white/neutral background. The current official SVG bundle uses browser-only foreignObject gradients, so the official PNG is used for reliable native rendering. No generated/retraced artwork.
- **Meta**: `meta-official.svg` contains the exact primary symbol from `Meta_Company Lockup/1 Positive Primary/RGB/Meta_lockup_positive primary_RGB.svg`, downloaded via the [Meta Brand Resource Center](https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/meta/company-brand/). The wordmark/artboard nodes are omitted and the viewBox is set to the supplied symbol clipping bounds; the symbol paths, raster gradient, colors and aspect ratio are unchanged. Meta integration remains marked demo/unavailable until its actual connector is implemented. Meta's published guidelines require approval for logo usage before public release; this asset does not establish that approval.
- **Apple**: The existing Apple mark is retained unchanged. Device permission controls below it are separate iOS permissions, not an Apple account login. See [Apple trademark guidelines](https://www.apple.com/legal/intellectual-property/guidelinesfor3rdparties.html) for public distribution review.
