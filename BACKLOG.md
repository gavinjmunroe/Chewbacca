## Source and status contract

`bin/backlog` selects the first existing BACKLOG.md under `CHEWBACCA_PRIVATE`, then `~/chewbacca-private`, then its own checkout. This run selected this checkout; because the CLI selects one store, future edits must recheck that selection. The private work ledger is a separate workspace-scoped commitment store, not a release log.

This is the canonical public request/status register. [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) orders bounded work; [docs/ROADMAP.md](docs/ROADMAP.md) maps the older product priorities here. Neither maintains a second status list. [Evidence](docs/BACKLOG-EVIDENCE.md) gives dated observations and their limits.

Stable identity is CB- plus the numeric first column (for example CB-0). Original numbers are retained. Legacy `30b` becomes CB-130; duplicate `51` splits into CB-51 (design sources) and CB-151 (humor). IDs never imply priority. Original wording, all three pre-edit documents, hashes, and private evidence references are preserved in the existing private context store under the reconciliation record dated 2026-10-02. Sensitive original content is deliberately absent here. Historical statements and original requests remain evidence, never new authorization.

`open`: defined work remains. `implemented`: inspected implementation exists, with acceptance still outstanding. `tested`: the stated bounded test passed in this reconciliation. `released`: the stated publicly accessible scope was observed. `unverified`: current acceptance has not been established, including old done claims. `blocked`: missing identified input prevents the next decision. `deferred`: preserved for a later batch, not canceled. `external`: owned by another current task; consume its receipt, do not edit or close it. Tested/released entries close only their narrow scopes. A parent stays open until all of its acceptance checks pass.

Evidence codes E0-E8 and P1/P2 are defined in the evidence document. H means a historical source claim, not current proof. Dependencies are prerequisites to full acceptance; an offline audit can begin before them. No edge grants sending, spending, publication, recording or permission changes.

## Direction

Preserve the ambition: expertise across domains, expertise at learning, acquiring new craft, executable skills, empirical improvement with retained alternatives, and teaching people to work independently. Success is a person becoming more capable, eventually without the tool. Corpus size, skill count, a framework or a passing unit suite cannot establish expertise. Lower-level adaptation, including local models and steering/weight-level research, remains a research possibility subject to defined tasks, budgets and the user's selected runtime.

The reconciliation uses a status table and explicit dependencies. A full graph database would add machinery without resolving more contradictions. The discriminating check is complete source-to-ID coverage, unique IDs, an acyclic dependency list, and no completion without a matching evidence scope. False closure and dropped requests cost more than retaining a visible unknown.
